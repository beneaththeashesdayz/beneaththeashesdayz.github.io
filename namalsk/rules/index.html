Player Handbook

Namalsk
Rules

This is a hardcore PvP server. There are no safe zones, no guaranteed fairness and no expectation of mercy. These rules exist to protect fair competition and prevent exploits—not to limit legitimate gameplay.

General Conduct

  • Respect other players and staff.
  • No racism, hate speech, threats, harassment or excessive toxicity.
  • No cheating, hacking, duping or exploiting bugs.
  • English only in global chat unless otherwise stated.
  • Staff decisions are final. If you disagree, open a Discord ticket.
  • Report exploits to staff. Abusing them will result in punishment.

In-Game Name & Discord Verification

  • Your in-game name must match your verified Discord name.
  • Alternate accounts are not permitted.
  • This is required for moderation, reports and support.
  • Failure to comply may result in removal from the server.

PvP Rules

  • Kill on sight is encouraged.
  • PvP is permitted anywhere on the map.
  • There are no safe zones.
  • No teaming or temporary alliances. Brief spontaneous non-hostile interactions are allowed, but players must remain independent.
  • Maximum three players per group.
  • Combat logging and raid logging are strictly prohibited.
  • After shooting or being shot, you may not combat log for five minutes.
  • If disconnected by a crash, restart or lost connection, log back in immediately.

Base Building

  • Maximum one territory per group.
  • Bases must have at least one and no more than six raidable doors or gates.
  • Do not block military locations, water fountains, major roads, map progression or event locations.
  • Do not build in police stations, hospitals, fire stations, civilian barracks or summer camps.
  • Do not build on roads.
  • Leave a player-wide gap between all walls and gates.
  • Floating or unrealistic bases are not permitted. Bases over water must remain connected to land.
  • Bases may extend a maximum of one tile beyond their foundation unless the overhang is realistically supported.
  • Place your flagpole at your own risk. No compensation is provided if another group removes your territory and claims the area.

Base Defenses

  • Bases must always be raidable from the ground.
  • Do not require helicopters or other unintended methods to access a base.
  • No building methods intended to create unraidable bases.
  • Do not exploit game mechanics for defensive advantage.
  • Use unobstructed windows; shooting through wall gaps is prohibited.
  • Windows may bypass doors only after every section is accessible and all raidable doors have been breached.
  • Do not force raiders into animations to reach loot or the base flag.
  • Tight shooting positions, horizontal windows and ceiling windows are prohibited.
  • Metal stairs may not be used to increase defensive advantage.

Base Raiding

  • Online and offline raiding are allowed.
  • Base raiding is encouraged.
  • No glitching, clipping or exploiting to gain access.
  • If you can legitimately raid it, you may take anything inside.

Taking Over Bases

  • A base is considered taken over when its flag is removed. Protect your flag at your own risk.
  • If giving away a base, fully leave before the new owners arrive. Meeting at the base would count as teaming.

Vehicles

  • Vehicle theft is allowed and encouraged.
  • If you can steal it, claim it or raid it, it is yours.
  • Vehicle recovery is part of PvP gameplay.

Fair Play

  • Play the game as intended.
  • If something clearly abuses game mechanics or third-party software to gain an unfair advantage, staff may intervene even if it is not specifically listed here.
