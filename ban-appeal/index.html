Beneath the Ashes // Player Support

Ban Appeal

Every appeal starts with your side of the story. Use this form to explain what happened and why you believe your ban should be reconsidered.

Before you submit

Help us review your appeal

Be honest, specific and respectful. Include the name you used when you were banned and any details that will help staff find the incident.

Read the rules for Chernarus or Nasdara before submitting.

Staff may contact you at the email address you provide. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that your ban will be lifted. Please allow time for review before sending another appeal.

If you were also banned from our Discord, you can still use this form.

Your appeal

Fields marked * are required.

Use your username, not only your display name. If you do not have Discord, enter “None.”
Open Steam → your profile → copy the profile URL. If you cannot access your account, explain here.
Use an address you can access so staff can respond.
Leave blank if you do not remember.
Copy the message if possible, or enter “Unknown.”
Explain any mistake, missing context, or what you would do differently if allowed to return.
Link screenshots, clips or other relevant evidence. Check that staff can open the links. Do not include passwords or other private account details.

Your submission is processed by FormSubmit and emailed to Beneath the Ashes staff for review.

Complete the spam verification on the next screen to finish submitting. If submission fails, keep a copy of your appeal and try again, or email beneaththeashesdayz@gmail.com.
