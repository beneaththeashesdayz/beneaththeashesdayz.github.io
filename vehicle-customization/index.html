01 // A New Coat of Paint

Custom Vehicle Retextures

$25Service price
Creation & implementation

Give your favorite vehicle a personalized texture based on your requested design.

Designs We Can Create

  • Solid colors — just about any color you can dream up.
  • Repeating patterns, including camouflage, florals, geometric designs and themed prints.
  • Custom color combinations and pattern variations.

Outside the Current Scope

  • Highly detailed custom artwork.
  • Complex, one-of-a-kind illustrations.
  • Elaborate vehicle designs requiring extensive custom artwork.

How It Works

  1. You must already own the vehicle in-game. Vehicles are not included with a custom texture request.
  2. Submit your requested color or repeating pattern through a Discord support ticket.
  3. Once completed, your custom texture will be added to our configured vehicle repainting system through LB Master’s Virtual Garages.
  4. Repaint your vehicle using the existing in-game mechanic, paying the applicable repainting cost with your character’s in-game currency.
What the $25 covers: creation and implementation of your custom texture. It does not include the vehicle or the separate in-game repainting cost.

02 // Leave Your Mark

Personalized License Plates

$5Service price
Creation & implementation

Because even the apocalypse deserves a little personalization. Put your Discord name, survivor nickname or a phrase of your own on a custom plate.

Plate Design Options

  • Custom text.
  • Any color combination.
  • Different plate styles and variants.
  • Personalized names, phrases and creative designs.

How It Works

  1. Submit your desired plate text, colors and preferred design or variant through a Discord support ticket.
  2. Our team creates your personalized license plate texture.
  3. Once completed, your custom plate will be added to the Vehicle Supplies Trader at Scorched Isle Market.
  4. Your character can then purchase the completed plate using in-game currency.
What the $5 covers: creation and implementation of the custom plate design. The plate itself must still be purchased in-game.
Check compatibility first. Custom license plates are currently functional only on select in-game vehicles. Not every vehicle supports this system. Open a support ticket before submitting your design so staff can investigate your vehicle’s compatibility.

03 // Bring Us Your Idea

Start With a Support Ticket

All custom vehicle retexture and license plate requests currently go through Discord support. Our staff will review your request, confirm compatibility and discuss the design with you.

Include These Details

  • Your vehicle’s make and model.
  • The customization you’re requesting.
  • Reference images, colors or repeating patterns you’d like us to use.
  • For plates, your preferred text, colors and plate style or variant.
  • Any additional details that help us understand your vision.
