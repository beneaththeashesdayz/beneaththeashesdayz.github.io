Shared Information // All Services

Customization Policies

These terms apply to vehicle retextures, personalized plates, gear retextures and weapon skins on Chernarus and Nasdara.

Customization charges and in-game items
All advertised service charges are in USD and cover the custom appearance, creation and implementation. They do not include the actual vehicle, gear, weapon or attachments. Obtain your base items through normal gameplay. In-game item prices and any repainting charges are separate from the USD service fee.
Compatibility and mod permissions
Not every item, vehicle, weapon part or plate system supports customization. Before accepting payment, staff confirm compatibility and verify that the relevant mod permits retexturing and monetized use. Custom appearances depend on the underlying item, compatible mods and the server's implementation.
One purchase, one selected server
Services are offered on Chernarus and Nasdara, but each purchase covers only the server selected in your ticket. Adding the same design to the other server is a new purchase at the standard service price.
Available through normal gameplay
Retextured items are made available to all players through normal gameplay on the selected server. Purchasing a design does not give you exclusive access to its in-game appearance.
Previews, approval and revisions
You review a preview and approve the design before implementation. As many design revisions as needed are included until implementation, including changes after approval but before implementation. Changes requested after implementation are not included and require a separate quote.
Timing for your design
Staff quote timing individually in your Discord ticket, depending on the complexity of the design. There is no single turnaround time for every request.
Reuse after loss, destruction or death
Reuse your vehicle design on a replacement of the same approved model, or your gear or weapon design on the same approved item, without another USD fee. Plate designs may be reused on another compatible vehicle. You must obtain the replacement item or plate through gameplay and pay any normal in-game acquisition or repainting costs.
Automatic availability after wipes
Purchased designs remain available automatically after server wipes, without another USD fee, as long as the approved item is still supported. You must still obtain the item in-game and pay normal in-game costs.
If an item or mod loses support
If a mod is removed, discontinued or becomes incompatible, we will offer to adapt the design to another supported item at no additional USD fee. The same option applies if an item unexpectedly proves incompatible after payment.
Cancellations and refunds
All customization payments are non-refundable. This applies before design work begins, during design work, after approval and after implementation. For unexpected incompatibility or a loss of mod support, see the design adaptation policy.
Artwork and content restrictions
No hateful, racist, sexually explicit or illegal content. You must own or have permission to use any artwork, logos or images you submit. Staff check the relevant mod's retexturing and monetized-use permissions before accepting payment.
Cosmetic appearance, not gameplay advantage
Custom appearances must not improve item statistics or provide an in-game advantage. The service buys a cosmetic design, not improved performance, equipment or currency. See Bohemia Interactive’s official monetization FAQ.
05 // Bring Us Your Idea

Start With a Support Ticket

All custom retexture, weapon skin and license plate requests for Chernarus and Nasdara currently go through Discord support. Our staff will review your request, confirm compatibility and explain the implementation process for your selected server.

  1. Open a customization ticket. Use the Discord support link below.
  2. Provide the item and design details. Include your server, exact item or model, references and any matching pieces.
  3. Confirm compatibility and pricing with staff. Staff check the exact items, mod permissions and design scope, then confirm the total price.
  4. Pay through the link provided in your ticket. Staff send a Tip4Server checkout link after confirming compatibility and pricing. Complete payment, then design work begins on the individually quoted schedule. All payments are non-refundable.
  5. Review and approve the design. Check the preview and confirm the final appearance.
  6. Implementation and in-game availability. Staff implement the approved appearance and explain how to access or apply it on the selected server.

Include These Details

  • Your server: Chernarus or Nasdara.
  • The exact item or weapon name; for vehicles and plates, include the vehicle make and model.
  • The customization you’re requesting and every gear piece or weapon attachment you’d like included.
  • Reference images, artwork, themes, colors or patterns you’d like us to use.
  • For plates, your desired text, colors and plate style or variant.
  • Any additional details that help us understand your vision.

Quick Answers // Before You Order

Frequently Asked Questions

Are customization purchases currently open?

Yes. Purchases are open for custom vehicle retextures, personalized license plates, gear retextures and weapon skins. All listed service prices are in USD.

How do I purchase a customization?

Open a Discord customization ticket with your server, item and design details. Staff confirm compatibility, mod permissions and pricing, then send a Tip4Server checkout link in the ticket. Payment comes before design work; you approve a preview before implementation.

What does the $25 USD vehicle service cover?

One custom design for one approved vehicle model. Model variants are not included. The actual vehicle and any in-game repainting costs are separate.

Does purchasing a customization include the in-game item?

No. The USD charge covers cosmetic customization, not the actual vehicle, gear, weapon or attachments. Obtain base items through normal gameplay; any in-game acquisition or repainting costs are separate.

Will I see my design before it goes live?

Yes. You review a preview and approve the design before staff implement it. See the preview and revision policy.

Can I request design changes?

As many revisions as needed are included until implementation, even after approval. Changes requested after implementation require a separate quote.

How long will my customization take?

Staff quote timing individually in your Discord ticket based on design complexity.

Can another player use my custom design?

Yes. Retextured items are available to all players through normal gameplay on the selected server. The design's in-game appearance is not exclusive to the buyer.

What happens if I lose my customized item?

You can reuse the design without another USD fee on a replacement of the same approved vehicle model, gear item or weapon. Plates can be reused on another compatible vehicle. Obtain the replacement item or plate in-game and pay normal in-game costs. See the reuse policy.

Will my customization survive a server wipe?

Yes. Designs remain available automatically without another USD fee while the approved item is supported. You still need to obtain the item in-game and pay normal in-game costs.

Can I use my customization on both BTA servers?

A purchase covers only the server selected in your ticket. Adding the same design to the other server requires a new purchase at the standard service price.

Can I customize any vehicle, weapon or piece of gear?

Compatibility must be checked for the exact item and server. Not every model, gear item, weapon part or plate system supports customization. Include the make and model for vehicle and plate requests, and identify each gear item or attachment.

What happens if a third-party mod is removed?

If the mod is removed, discontinued or becomes incompatible, we offer to adapt your design to another supported item at no additional USD fee. See the mod support policy.

Are matching weapon attachments included?

The $15 USD weapon skin covers the weapon design. One matching attachment set costs an additional $15 USD and covers one approved, compatible attachment per supported slot on the selected weapon. The combined design price is $30 USD; actual in-game weapons and attachments are not included.

What happens if my requested item cannot be customized?

Staff check compatibility and mod permissions before accepting payment. If an item unexpectedly proves incompatible after payment, we offer to adapt the design to another supported item at no additional USD fee.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

All customization payments are non-refundable, including before work begins. This remains the policy during design work, after approval and after implementation. Unexpected incompatibility is handled through the design adaptation policy.
