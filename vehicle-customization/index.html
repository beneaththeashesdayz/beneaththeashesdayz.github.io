01 // A New Coat of Paint

Custom Vehicle Retextures

$25Service price
Creation & implementation

Give your favorite vehicle a personalized texture based on your requested design.

Designs We Can Create

  • Solid colors and custom color combinations.
  • Patterns, including camouflage, florals, geometric designs and themed prints.
  • Detailed custom artwork and one-of-a-kind illustrations.
  • Full vehicle themes, original designs and elaborate custom liveries.

How It Works

  1. You must already own the vehicle in-game. Vehicles are not included with a custom texture request.
  2. Submit your design idea and any reference images, artwork, colors or patterns through a Discord support ticket.
  3. Our team creates and implements your texture, then confirms the in-game repainting process for your selected server.
  4. Follow the instructions in your ticket to apply the texture. Staff will confirm any applicable repainting cost in in-game currency.
What the $25 covers: creation and implementation of your custom texture. It does not include the vehicle or any separate in-game repainting cost.

02 // Leave Your Mark

Personalized License Plates

$5Service price
Creation & implementation

Because even the apocalypse deserves a little personalization. Put your Discord name, survivor nickname or a phrase of your own on a custom plate.

Plate Design Options

  • Custom text.
  • Any color combination.
  • Different plate styles and variants.
  • Personalized names, phrases and creative designs.

How It Works

  1. Submit your desired plate text, colors and preferred design or variant through a Discord support ticket.
  2. Our team creates your personalized license plate texture.
  3. Staff will confirm how the completed plate will be made available on your selected server.
  4. Follow the instructions in your ticket to obtain the plate. Staff will confirm any separate purchase cost in in-game currency.
What the $5 covers: creation and implementation of the custom plate design. Any separate in-game purchase cost is not included.
Check compatibility first. Custom license plates are currently functional only on select in-game vehicles. Not every vehicle supports this system. Open a support ticket before submitting your design so staff can investigate your vehicle’s compatibility.

05 // Bring Us Your Idea

Start With a Support Ticket

All custom retexture, weapon skin and license plate requests for Chernarus and Nasdara currently go through Discord support. Our staff will review your request, confirm compatibility and explain the implementation process for your selected server.

Include These Details

  • Your server: Chernarus or Nasdara.
  • The exact item or weapon name; for vehicles, include the make and model.
  • The customization you’re requesting and every piece you’d like included.
  • Reference images, artwork, themes, colors or patterns you’d like us to use.
  • For plates, your preferred text, colors and plate style or variant.
  • Any additional details that help us understand your vision.
