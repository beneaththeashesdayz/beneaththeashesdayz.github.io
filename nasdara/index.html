NASDARA

A new world. New dangers. A different fight for survival.

Welcome to Nasdara, a Beneath the Ashes PvE survival experience where danger doesn't always announce itself.

Beyond the abandoned roads and forgotten settlements lies a frontier shaped by desperate survivors, underground trade, organized threats, and secrets buried long before the world fell apart.

The markets are open. The armed patrols are watching. And somewhere beyond the safety of civilization, something is still operating.

Welcome to Nasdara. Your story begins here.
