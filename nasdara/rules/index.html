The Laws
of Nasdara

Official Server Rules & Community Guidelines

Even where civilization has fallen, order must remain. Nasdara is a place for survivors to explore, build, trade, and endure the dangers of a broken world together. These regulations protect that experience and ensure every survivor has a fair opportunity to thrive.

Primary Server Policy

BTA Nasdara is a 100% PvE server.

Player-versus-player combat, raiding, theft, and deliberate damage to other players or their property are strictly prohibited throughout the map.

There are no exceptions allowing PvP or raiding. The limited permission to loot eligible grave crosses is explained in Section 2.

01 General Conduct

  • Treat players and staff respectfully.
  • Racism, harassment, hate speech, and excessive toxicity are prohibited.
  • Cheating, exploiting, glitch abuse, duplication, and unauthorized third-party software are forbidden.
  • Report discovered exploits to staff. Deliberate exploitation may result in a permanent ban.
  • Use English in global chat unless staff announce otherwise.
  • In-game names must match Discord names to support identification and ticket handling.
  • Staff decisions are final.

02 PvE & Player Protection

  • Shooting, attacking, intentionally damaging, or killing other players is prohibited.
  • Teamkilling is prohibited.
  • Base raiding and base theft are prohibited anywhere on Nasdara, regardless of territory claims, flags or flagpoles, locks, accessibility, or apparent abandonment. See Section 03.
  • Stealing from player-owned storage, vehicles, tents, or containers is prohibited.
  • Griefing, harassment, blocking access, and deliberately attracting infected toward other players or their bases are prohibited.
  • Grave crosses may be looted unless they are inside another player's territory or an area designated by staff as a Safe Zone. This permission applies only to eligible grave crosses; it does not permit theft from protected storage, vehicles, tents, or containers.

No PvP. No raiding. No theft of protected property. These protections apply throughout the map, regardless of territory claim or flag status. The grave-cross exception above applies only to eligible grave crosses; it is not permission to enter another player’s secured base, dismantle structures, or take items from player bases, storage containers, or protected property.

03 Building & Territories

Base Raiding & Theft: Zero Tolerance

Base raiding, base theft, and unauthorized interference with player-built structures are STRICTLY PROHIBITED anywhere on Nasdara.

All player-built bases, structures, and stored property are protected throughout the entire map. This protection applies regardless of:

  • Whether a base has an active territory claim.
  • Whether a territory flag or flagpole is present.
  • Whether a flagpole is constructed, damaged, missing, or otherwise nonfunctional.
  • Whether the base appears occupied, abandoned, unfinished, or inactive.
  • Whether doors, gates, storage containers, or other structures are locked or unlocked.
  • Whether the base is actively maintained or appears to have been left unattended.

AN UNCLAIMED BASE IS NOT AN UNPROTECTED BASE.

AN UNLOCKED CONTAINER IS NOT AN INVITATION TO STEAL.

AN ABANDONED-LOOKING BASE IS NOT FREE PROPERTY.

Players are strictly prohibited from:

  • Entering another player's secured base without permission.
  • Removing items from another player's base or storage containers.
  • Dismantling, destroying, damaging, or modifying another player's structures.
  • Removing locks, gates, doors, flags, or other base components.
  • Exploiting missing territory claims, inaccessible flagpoles, or other technical issues to gain unauthorized access.
  • Claiming, occupying, or taking ownership of another player's existing base without staff authorization.

Suspected Abandoned Bases

If you discover a base you believe has been abandoned, you must submit a Discord support ticket for staff review.

Only authorized server staff may determine whether a base is abandoned and eligible for removal or cleanup. Players may not independently declare another player's base abandoned or claim its contents.

Authorized staff cleanup or enforcement is separate from player raiding. It does not grant players permission to take a base or its contents.

Building Restrictions & Territory Limits

  • One permanent base is permitted per solo player or group.
  • Hidden supply stashes are allowed.
  • A temporary second territory flag is permitted exclusively for relocating an existing base.
  • Remove the original or temporary territory within 48 hours of placing the temporary second flag.
  • Do not block public roads, water sources, essential facilities, or access to important gameplay locations.
  • Do not obstruct traders or prevent other players from using public services.
  • No sky bases or unsupported floating structures.
  • Avoid excessive wall stacking or extremely large structures that negatively affect performance.
  • Staff may require changes to builds that cause server instability.
  • Structures violating building regulations may be removed without reimbursement.

04 Vehicles & Property

  • All player-owned vehicles are protected throughout the map.
  • Vehicle theft, lockpicking, deliberate damage, unauthorized inventory access, and attempts to bypass ownership are prohibited.
  • Do not obstruct roads or public facilities with parked vehicles.
  • Players are responsible for maintaining spare vehicle keys.
  • Staff are not responsible for lost keys or player mistakes.
  • Loot cycling is prohibited.

05 Safe Zones & Trading

These trading protections apply across Nasdara. Safe Zone restrictions apply to any area staff designate as a Safe Zone.

  • In any area designated by staff as a Safe Zone, theft, trolling, harassment, and trap placement are prohibited.
  • Do not take another player's dropped items without permission.
  • Respect player transactions and trading activities.
  • Do not obstruct traders, trading stations, or public interaction points.

06 Admin Support & Reimbursements

Staff May Assist With

  • Confirmed server bugs.
  • Mod-related errors.
  • Players trapped in inaccessible map objects or structures.
  • Investigating rule violations.
  • Resolving server-side technical problems.

Staff Are Not Responsible For

  • Lost keys.
  • Accidental item drops.
  • Player negligence.
  • Problems players can reasonably resolve using available gameplay tools.
  • Items lost through normal interactions with placed objects.
  • Vehicle recovery when functioning in-game recovery tools are available.

Vehicle Replacement Policy

Vehicles may be replaced or repaired only when a confirmed server crash, mod error, or server-side bug caused the loss.

Replacements remain subject to staff verification. Lost keys and player mistakes do not qualify.

07 Enforcement & Penalties

Base raiding, base theft, and unauthorized interference with player-built structures are rule violations handled under the existing disciplinary progression and staff discretion below.

The standard disciplinary progression is:

  1. First OffenseWarning
  2. Second OffenseTemporary Ban
  3. Third OffensePermanent Ban

Severe violations, including cheating, duplication, serious harassment, and deliberate exploitation, may result in immediate permanent bans without prior warnings.

Staff retain discretion based on the seriousness and circumstances of each violation.
