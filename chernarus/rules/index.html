Player Handbook

Server
Rules

Even in the ashes, order remains. These rules protect the survival experience for everyone on Chernarus. Read them before you play, build, trade or enter a PvP zone.

Important update:
PvP zones are no longer raid zones. Vehicle theft is permitted only inside designated PvP zones.

General Conduct

  • Respect all players and admins.
  • Racism, harassment, hate speech and excessive toxicity are not allowed.
  • Exploits, glitches, duping and cheating are prohibited.
  • Use English in global chat unless otherwise stated.
  • In-game names must match your Discord name so staff can identify you for tickets, reports and support.
  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Report exploits to staff. Abusing an exploit instead of reporting it may result in punishment.

PvE Rules

  • Base raiding is not allowed in PvE areas.
  • Do not steal from player bases, vehicles or tents in PvE areas.
  • Do not grief players by following, blocking or intentionally baiting zombies into bases.
  • Grave crosses may be looted in PvE areas unless they are inside a Safe Zone or a player Territory Radius.

PvP Zones

  • Kill on sight is allowed inside designated PvP zones.
  • Stealing and destruction are allowed inside PvP zones.
  • PvP zones are not raid zones. Base raiding is not permitted simply because a base is inside a PvP zone.
  • Vehicle lockpicking and vehicle theft are permitted only inside designated PvP zones.
  • Entering a PvP zone is at your own risk. Staff will not assist with deaths, lost gear or disputes that occur there.
  • Combat logging is prohibited.

Building Rules

  • Do not build inside hospitals, medical buildings or police stations.
  • Do not build inside the Mine.
  • Do not block access to the Mine, Bunker, Banks, ATMs, roads or water pumps.
  • Do not block roaming traders or prevent players from interacting with them.
  • One base per group. Stash spots are allowed.
  • A temporary second territory flag may be used only while relocating. Remove the original or temporary flag within 48 hours of placing the new flag.
  • No sky bases or floating structures.
  • No excessive wall stacking or lag-causing builds.
  • If your base causes lag, you may be required to fix it. Failure to comply may result in administrative action.
  • Structures that violate these restrictions may be removed without reimbursement.

Vehicles & Loot

Everyday Vehicle Rules

  • Do not block roads with vehicles.
  • Do not leave vehicles at Scorched Isle Market under any circumstances. Vehicles left there may be deleted by Safe Zone cleanup after 30 minutes and will not be reimbursed by admins.
  • Store vehicles in a garage when logging off.
  • Vehicles left unused for six hours may be impounded.
  • Loot cycling is not allowed.

Protected Vehicles

Vehicles parked in Safe Zones, inside an active player territory or anywhere in a PvE area are protected.

Do not lockpick, steal, damage, access the inventory of, or attempt to bypass ownership of protected vehicles.

Vehicle Theft in PvP Zones

  • Vehicle lockpicking and theft are allowed only inside designated PvP zones.
  • Nearby players may defend, attack or contest the vehicle.
  • Do not repeatedly target the same player or group with the sole intent of harassment or disruption.

Vehicle Replacement

Vehicles are replaced, repaired or restored only for confirmed server crashes, confirmed mod issues or verified server-side bugs.

Lost keys, player mistakes and PvP losses do not qualify.

Vehicle Keys

  • Make spare keys for all vehicles.
  • Lost keys are the owner’s responsibility.
  • Staff will not unlock vehicles or replace vehicle keys due to player error.

Traders & Safe Zones

  • No stealing or trolling.
  • Do not pick up items on the ground that do not belong to you.
  • Items inside trash cans are considered abandoned and may be taken.

Admin Support

Staff Can Help With

  • Server bugs.
  • Players stuck inside mapped objects or base-built walls and structures.
  • Clearing confirmed rule violations.

Staff Cannot Help With

  • Deaths or gear lost in PvP zones.
  • Players who are stuck in a location they could reasonably leave themselves.
  • Player mistakes.
  • Flipped vehicles when in-game recovery tools are available.
  • Items lost while interacting with placeable objects such as tables, stations or tents.

Enforcement

  • First offense: Warning
  • Second offense: Temporary ban
  • Third offense: Permanent ban
  • Severe violations may skip steps at admin discretion.
