Preservation

Turn your haul into canned meals, fruit preserves and pickled jars with the Chef’s Table on Beneath the Ashes Chernarus.

What you need

Your basic shopping list is a Chef’s Table kit, an empty can or jar, a preserving ingredient, and enough raw food of one type. A cooking pot and the English Chef’s Cookbook are useful extras.

Chef’s Table kits

Search industrial, farm, and village buildings. Kits spawn as floor loot.

Empty cans and jars

Find them as shelf loot in town, village and farm buildings.

Salt, sugar, and vinegar

Find them as shelf loot in town, village and farm buildings. Vinegar may be labelled Ocet.

English Chef’s Cookbook

Search shelves in town and village buildings. It is a handy reference, but you do not need it attached to the table to make the recipes below.

These are eligible building types across Chernarus, with no loot-tier restrictions or fixed collection points. Finding a suitable building does not guarantee an item will be there.

The finished preserved foods do not spawn as ground loot. Gather your supplies and make them yourself.

Set up your table

  1. Find a Chef’s Table kit and bring it to a suitable spot where server building rules allow placement.
  2. Hold the kit and use its placement action to position and deploy the table.
  3. Open the deployed table’s inventory. Use the dedicated attachment slots for supplies and food.
Attachments matter. Dropping ingredients into the table’s ordinary storage grid does not count as loading the recipe slots.

The table can display a pot, cookbook, and alarm clock, but the canning actions checked for this guide do not require those extras. No power supply, fuel, or lit fire is required by these recipes.

Step 1: Prepare the container

Canning happens in two separate steps. First you prepare an empty container; then you use that prepared container with food.

  1. Attach an empty can or jar to its empty-container slot.
  2. Attach salt, sugar, or vinegar to the matching supply slot.
  3. Stand close to the table, aim at it, and select the action to prepare the appropriate can or jar.
  4. Collect the prepared container that appears on or beside the tabletop.

Empty can + saltFor meat, fish, lard, or potatoes.

Empty can + sugarFor apples, pears, plums, sliced pumpkin, or tomatoes.

Empty can + vinegarFor green bell peppers.

Empty jar + sugarFor jam and fruit purées.

Empty jar + vinegarFor pickled produce and mushrooms.

Each preparation uses 10% of a full salt packet, sugar packet, or vinegar bottle under our current settings. A partially used packet or bottle must still contain enough for that preparation.

Step 2: Add food and finish the recipe

  1. Put the prepared container into the matching prepared-can or prepared-jar slot. This is a different slot from the empty-container slot.
  2. Load raw food into the table’s three food attachment slots. Every occupied food slot must contain the same type of food.
  3. Make sure the combined quantity is enough for the chosen recipe.
  4. Aim at the table and select the action for that food.
  5. Collect your finished product from the tabletop area.

One completed recipe makes one finished can or jar. The prepared container is consumed, along with the required amount of food. Any excess food quantity is left over.

Do not cook the ingredients first. These recipes check for raw food. Cooked, dried, burned, or rotten food will not satisfy that check.

For example: prepare a can with salt, attach that prepared can in the canning section, add enough raw chicken breast to the food slots, and use the chicken-canning action.

What can you preserve?

Salted cans: meat and fish

Use one food type per batch:

  • Chicken, rabbit, sheep, goat, cow, pig, boar
  • Deer, reindeer, fox, wolf, bear
  • Carp, mackerel, steelhead trout, walleye pollock, sardines
  • Lard

Use the exact supported meat or fish type. Other fish supplied by additional mods are not automatically compatible.

Salted cans: potatoes

Use raw potatoes with a can prepared with salt.

Sugared cans

Apples, pears, plums, sliced pumpkin, or tomatoes.

Tomatoes use sugar in the current recipe code. If an older cookbook description says vinegar, follow this guide for the current BTA setup.

Vinegar cans

Green bell peppers.

Sugared jars

Apples, pears, plums, sliced pumpkin, rose hips, or elderberries.

Vinegar jars

Pears, green bell peppers, zucchini, or sliced pumpkin.

Supported mushrooms: field mushrooms, oyster mushrooms, saffron milk caps, penny buns, and parasol mushrooms.

Some recipes depend on food that may be harder to obtain on Chernarus. Recipe support does not mean every ingredient has a natural spawn on this map.

Not enough food? Bring a cooking pot

Recipes use food quantity, not simply the number of pieces. Three small or partly eaten pieces may still be insufficient.

The mod supports combining suitable food portions inside a vanilla cooking pot. Hold the pot in your hands, place matching portions inside it, and use the combine function. Move the combined food back into the table’s food slots afterward.

Keep each batch to one exact food type. Mixing chicken and pork, or different mushroom species, will stop the corresponding table recipe from appearing.

The pot helps prepare your portions; you do not need to light a fire or cook the food in it before canning.

If the crafting action does not appear

  1. Check the container stage. An empty container must be prepared before it can be used with food.
  2. Check the slots. Use the dedicated supply, prepared-container, and food attachment slots, not the ordinary cargo grid.
  3. Check the preserving ingredient. Salt, sugar, and vinegar serve different recipes. Potatoes use salt; tomatoes use sugar.
  4. Check food condition and type. Use raw ingredients of one exact supported type. Clear unrelated food from the other food slots.
  5. Check quantity. Add more of the same food or combine small portions in a pot. Also check how much salt, sugar, or vinegar remains.
  6. Check your position. Stand close to the table, aim directly at it, and cycle through the available actions.

If you are still stuck, take a screenshot of the table’s attachment slots and contact staff through the community’s normal support channel. Include which recipe you were attempting.

Moving your table and storing your food

A deployed table cannot be picked up directly. Remove its attachments and clear out your belongings, then use a screwdriver or pliers to dismantle it back into a table kit. The tool takes wear.

Storage shelves are part of the mod, but shelf kits are not currently enabled as world loot. You do not need a shelf to use the canning table.

Store finished food securely and check each item’s normal opening, condition, and food information before eating. Do not assume opened food lasts forever or that every container will automatically be returned after eating.
