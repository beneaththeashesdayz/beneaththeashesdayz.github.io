Before You Start

  • Close DayZ completely before updating.
  • Keep Steam open and signed in.
  • Check #mod-updates for the exact mod named in the server notice.

Follow the instructions for the launcher you use. Start with Discord, then choose one of the two launcher walkthroughs below.

Check Discord for Mod Updates

When a server mod changes, the update notice is posted in Discord. Read the notice before launching so you know which files need attention.

  1. Step 01

    Open #mod-updates

    Open #mod-updates in the Beneath the Ashes Discord.

  2. Step 02

    Read the bot notification

    Use the bot notice to identify the mod that needs refreshing, then follow the launcher instructions on this page.

DZSA Launcher

Using DZSA? Verify the affected mod, then refresh the server list and reconnect.

  1. Step 01

    Open the Mods tab

    Open the Mods tab.

  2. Step 02

    Find the affected mod

    Find the mod listed in #mod-updates.

  3. Step 03

    Verify the mod

    Click its circular-arrow Verify button.

  1. Step 04

    Refresh & rejoin

    Open Servers, search for Beneath the Ashes, refresh, and join.

Official DayZ Launcher

Using the official launcher? Find the affected mod in Mods and use Repair, then refresh the server list and reconnect.

  1. Step 01

    Open the Mods tab

    Open the Mods tab from the left.

  2. Step 02

    Search for the affected mod

    Search for the affected mod.

  3. Step 03

    Identify outdated or missing mods

    Find the outdated or missing mod in the filtered list. Check for red updated labels shown in the screenshot.

  4. Step 04

    Repair the affected mod

    Right-click outdated or missing mods and choose Repair.

  5. Step 05

    Refresh & play

    Open Servers, refresh the list, select Beneath the Ashes, and choose Play.

Still Having Trouble Connecting?

Advanced troubleshooting — affected mod only. If verifying or repairing the affected mod doesn’t resolve your connection issue, you may need to refresh its Steam Workshop subscription.

  1. Close DayZ and your launcher.
  2. Unsubscribe from the affected mod in Steam Workshop, then subscribe again.
  3. Allow Steam to finish downloading the mod completely before attempting to reconnect.
