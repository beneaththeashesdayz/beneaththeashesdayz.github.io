Vehicles & Ownership

Vehicle
Keys

Pair a new key to your vehicle, create backups for your group, and keep your fleet accessible when it matters.

Pairing a Master Key

01 · Take a brand-new key

Use a fresh, unpaired vehicle key. It must not already belong to another vehicle.

02 · Look at the vehicle

Stand at the vehicle you want to secure and make sure you have the key ready.

03 · Hold the pairing prompt

Use the [F] Pair Key interaction and hold the prompt until the progress completes.

04 · Confirm “Master Key”

The key name should change to Master Key. That confirms the pairing succeeded.

If a freshly paired key does not become a Master Key, open a support ticket so staff can investigate.

Before You Start

  • Use a brand-new, unpaired key.
  • Make sure you are pairing it to the correct vehicle.
  • Hold the interaction until it finishes.
  • Confirm the key name changes before leaving.
