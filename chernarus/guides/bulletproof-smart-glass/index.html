Building & Defense

Bulletproof
& Smart Glass

See threats coming while protecting your base. Learn the difference between Bulletproof Glass and toggleable Smart Glass, then choose the right crafting method.

Bulletproof Glass

  • Transparent and protective: stops bullets while preserving visibility.
  • Available as half-height and full-height panels.
  • Snaps cleanly and centers into BaseBuildingPlus wall frames.
  • Designed to give defenders visibility without sacrificing protection.

Smart Glass

  • Electrochromic glass that can switch between clear and opaque.
  • Supply power through PowerGrid to toggle privacy mode.
  • Wire it to a switch for on-demand control.
  • Available in half and full height, with the same bulletproof protection.

How to Craft It

Option 1 · Hand Crafting

1 Glass Bottle + Blowtorch→1 Glass Mini Panel
5 Glass Mini Panels + Blowtorch→1 Glass Panel
1 Glass Panel + Hand Saw→Glass Kit (Half)
2 Glass Panels + Hand Saw→Glass Kit (Full)

Option 2 · BBP Workbench Book

Use nails, planks and a metal plate, with a hand saw and BBP tape measure as the required tools.

Placing & Dismantling

Placement: A green marker shows which side faces inside during placement.
Dismantling: Use a hammer strictly from the inside.
Recovery: Dismantling drops the panels so they can be rebuilt.
Security: Outsiders cannot dismantle your glass.
Build smart, survive smarter: place the panels with the interior marker facing the correct direction before finalizing the structure.
