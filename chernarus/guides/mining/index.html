Mining & Refining

Mining
Guide

Mine raw materials, refine metals, cut gemstones and combine them into increasingly valuable bars and tools.

Furnace Setup

Place your Furnace Kit and attach a Propane Gas Canister, Propane Connection Pipe and Wooden Crate.

Using the Furnace

  • Mine a node to receive its stone.
  • Place the stone in the furnace input slot.
  • Turn on the furnace.
  • Collect the refined nugget.

Attachments

Propane Gas Canister · Propane Connection Pipe · Wooden Crate

Anvil Setup

Place your Anvil Kit and attach a Hammer (FreezeHammer) and Wooden Crate.

Using the Anvil

  • Smelt stone in the Furnace to produce nuggets.
  • Gather 10 nuggets of the same metal.
  • Hold them in your hands and hover over the Anvil.
  • Hold Left Click when the crafting prompt appears.

Attachments

Hammer · Wooden Crate

Grinding Stone Setup

Place your Grinding Stone Kit and prepare the Grinding Stone Wheel and Wooden Crate attachments.

Using the Grinder

  • Mine a gemstone node to receive an uncut gem.
  • Place the uncut gem into the Grinding Stone.
  • Begin grinding to produce a cut gem.

Attachments

Grinding Stone Wheel · Wooden Crate

Grinding Recipes

Grinding an uncut gem can produce Flawed, Standard or Perfect quality. The available gemstone families include:

  • Amethyst · Jade · Amber · Iolite
  • Turquoise · Aquamarine · Fancy Colour Diamond
For each gemstone, use the corresponding Uncut gem in the Grinding Stone to produce its Flawed, Standard or Perfect result.

Mining Progression

Start by refining raw stone into nuggets, use the Anvil to create metal bars, cut gemstones with the Grinding Stone, then work upward through the Fusion Bar tiers. Better tools and higher-quality gems unlock the most advanced recipes.
