Survivor Field Guide

Hunting
Collectables

Trading cards, collector packs and rare memorabilia are scattered across Chernarus. Learn how to find them, complete collections and turn your finished sets into rewards.

Why Collect?

Collectables can be found in abandoned clothing and gear, carried by the infected, or hidden inside loot crates and supply caches. Every card brings you closer to a complete collection, while unopened packs offer the chance to discover something rare.

Collector mindset: search every corner, keep your finds organized and avoid breaking apart a collection before it is ready to be completed.

DayZ Collector Cards

1.Obtain card packs. Find packs in towns and on infected.
2.Assemble the DayZ Card Book. Acquire a card book and insert collected cards into its pages.
3.Complete the collection. Fill every slot with a unique card.
4.Submit a support ticket. Open a Discord ticket to arrange the completed book turn-in with an admin.

Fallout Collections

Fallout collectables are organized into three ten-card sets:

  • Pinup Set: purple cards.
  • Sci-Fi Set: red cards.
  • Western Set: blue cards.

Completion Protocol

  • Store each set in its matching colored lunchbox.
  • Seal the completed collection. Sealed boxes cannot be reopened.
  • The lunchbox will despawn and drop the Fallout Collectables at your feet.
  • Sell to a Collectable Trader, with Cinder Market offering the premium payout.
  • Check the Achievements menu to claim the completed collection reward.

Pokémon Collections

1.Find card packs. Search across the Chernarus map.
2.Collect and store cards. Keep them together in the collection box.
3.Seal the completed set. Sealed boxes cannot be opened again.
4.Sell the sealed box. Any Collectable Trader can accept it; Cinder Market pays the maximum reward.
5.Claim the achievement. Use the Achievements menu to collect your reward.

Where to Turn In

Authorized Collectable Traders can accept completed collections. For the best payout, bring sealed Fallout and Pokémon collections to the Collectable Trader at Cinder Market.

DayZ Card Book turn-ins are arranged through a Discord support ticket with an administrator.

Collector's Reminder

A true collector does not just survive the apocalypse. They preserve what remains of its history, one card at a time.
