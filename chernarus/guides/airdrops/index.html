Dynamic Events

Airdrops

Learn how specialized resupply drops work, what each drop contains, and which keys or tools you need to open them across Chernarus.

Resupply Drops

Redline Medical Resupply Drop

Packed with specialized medical supplies, including items you will not normally find out in the world.

Requires: Redline Medical Access Key. Keys can be found on zombies.

Ironline Basebuilding Resupply Drop

Loaded with construction materials, tools, fortification supplies and other goodies for keeping your corner of Chernarus standing.

Requires: Ironline Basebuilding Access Key. Keys can be found on zombies.

Remnant Combat Resupply Drop

A secured military resupply packed with weapons, ammunition, tactical equipment and battlefield supplies.

Requires: Remnant Combat Access Key. Keys can be found on zombies.

Sparky's Garage Resupply

Rare drops containing vehicles.

Requires: A lockpick. No special access key needed.

Personal Flares

Rare specialized airdrop flares allow you to call in your own Redline, Ironline or Remnant resupply drop instead of waiting for one to appear naturally.

Remember: Calling in an airdrop does not unlock it. You still need the corresponding access key to get inside.

Quick Checklist

  • Loot the dead.
  • Hang onto your access keys.
  • Keep your eyes open for rare flares.
  • Keep a lockpick when possible.
