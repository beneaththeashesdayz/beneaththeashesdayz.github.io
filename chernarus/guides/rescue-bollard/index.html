Vehicle Recovery

Rescue
Bollard

Recover the last vehicle you drove when it becomes stuck, overturned or otherwise inaccessible.

How It Works

01 · Deploy: Remove the Rescue Bollard from your inventory and place it on solid ground. Your vehicle will be pulled to this exact position.
02 · Register: Face the deployed bollard. It automatically registers the last vehicle you actively drove—not a vehicle you were only riding in as a passenger.
03 · Create Distance: Move away from the bollard to give the incoming vehicle clearance and reduce the risk of collision damage.
04 · Activate: Press [F6] to trigger the rescue sequence. The bollard pulls your registered vehicle to its location.

When to Use It

  • Your vehicle has fallen into a ravine.
  • The vehicle has flipped on rough terrain.
  • The vehicle is wedged in a tight or inaccessible position.
  • Manual recovery would take too long or is not possible.

Important Details

  • Choose the bollard’s placement carefully before registering the vehicle.
  • Only the most recent vehicle you operated as the driver is tracked.
  • Move clear before activating the rescue sequence.
  • Use the tool to recover your own inaccessible vehicle rather than abandoning it.
Plan the landing spot: place the bollard somewhere open and accessible. The rescued vehicle will arrive at the bollard, not beside you.

Quick Reference

Place the bollard → face it to register your last driven vehicle → move clear → press [F6].
