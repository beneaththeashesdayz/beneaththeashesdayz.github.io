Crafting & Customization

Weapons
Workbench

Use the workbench to craft weapon parts, paint supported firearms, convert calibers and apply vinyl wraps. Available options depend on the weapon and the current workbench configuration.

Important: This guide applies to Harald's Weapons only. Workbench recipes and supported weapons from other packs may differ.

Workbench Basics

Place the firearm and required materials into the workbench. If the recipe is available, it will appear as an action. Not every gun supports every option yet, so check the available weapon lists below.

Requirements vary: some actions require a screwdriver, pliers, a kit, spraycans or a specific firearm part.

Caliber Conversion

Requirements: Pliers, screwdriver and caliber conversion kit.

Supported calibers shown in the workbench include 5.56 NATO, .308 Win and .338 LM.

Vinyl Gun Wraps

Requirement: Vinyl Gun Wrap Kit.

Available wrap patterns include Dreamy, Cats, Nova, Paws, Shells, Zap, Bones, MultiCam, SnowCamo and LeafCamo.

Supported Guns

Honeybadger · AWM · MCX · MK14 · FN2000 · Malyuk Vulcan

Expanded Workbench List

Current wrap options also include MCX, MK14 EBR, FN2000, AWM, Malyuk Vulcan and HoneyBadger, with pattern availability varying by weapon.

Before You Start

Bring the correct kit and tools, verify that the weapon is supported, and look for the workbench action after placing the materials. If no action appears, that weapon or combination may not be available yet.
