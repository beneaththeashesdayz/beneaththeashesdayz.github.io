How the DNA system works

Choose either workbench route. Use the public Electronics Workbench at Cinder Market, or assemble your own personal bench. The card recipe, programming, activation, repair, and strongroom requirements are the same for both options.

The personal-workbench route

DNA is a crafting-and-looting progression. Build an Electronics Workbench, find a packaged chip, make and activate a matching keycard, then take it to a coloured SAFESTORE strongroom.

The basic route: bench parts → assembled bench → Blank KeyCard + packaged colour chip → chip installation → programming → activation → matching room.

Card colours & progression

There are five room tiers: Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple → Red. You do not have to clear every lower tier to craft a higher-tier card. The chip you use determines the card colour. A yellow card does not upgrade itself into a green card.

Fully working cards, inactive cards, unprogrammed cards, loose chips and DNA Security Keys are not normal ground loot in this setup. You find packaged chips and crafting supplies instead. Blank card bodies are an intentional exception: they are available as crafting materials.

Public DNA Workbench — Cinder Market

Cinder Market • Southern Sea

Not interested in gathering everything needed to construct your own Electronics Workbench? The Cinder Market has you covered!

Survivors can find a fully functional public Electronics Workbench at the Cinder Market, ready to assist with their DNA keycard operations. You do not have to construct your own workbench to participate in the DNA system.

Bring your required materials, chips, blank keycards, and supplies to use the workbench for crafting, programming, and restoring malfunctioning keycards.

Build your Electronics Workbench

1. Prepare the placement kit

Find a Workbench Kit, or combine 1 Metal Wire and 4 Short Sticks to craft one. Use the correct quantities; there is no need to risk an oversized stick stack.

Attach 1 Solder Station Kit and 1 Helping Hands Kit to it. Both must be attached before you can place it. Choose a suitable location that follows the server’s building rules, then deploy the kit.

2. Finish the incomplete bench

Attach all of the following to the placed Incomplete Electronics Workbench:

  • 5 Metal Sheets
  • 13 Wooden Planks
  • 16 Bolts
  • 1 Metal Wire
  • 1 Laptop
  • 1 Lockout Door Reader

Hold a usable Hammer, look at the incomplete bench and complete Assemble Workbench. Fill the material slots to the required quantities; one partial stack will not satisfy the check.

If you crafted the placement kit yourself, budget 2 Metal Wire in total: one for crafting the kit and another for the bench construction.

3. Stock the finished bench

Add a charged 40v Battery, Solder, Flux and the chip you want to install. The bench drawer must be closed during chip removal, chip installation and programming. Open it to organise supplies, then close it before using the work areas.

Craft one complete, usable keycard

These steps work at either finished Electronics Workbench. Use the public bench at Cinder Market or your own assembled bench. You only need the workbench construction materials when building your own.

Your per-card checklist

Once the bench is built, one new card needs:

  • 1 Blank KeyCard
  • 1 chip of the desired colour, unpacked from its blister pack
  • At least 20 Solder quantity
  • At least 20 Flux quantity
  • A charged 40v Battery with enough power for both operations

A full solder stack and a full flux bottle each contain 100 quantity, enough for five chip installations if no material has already been used. The chip is consumed. The blank body becomes the finished card; you do not need a separate blank card for the programming step.

Step A — install the chip

  1. Unpack the blister pack for the colour you want.
  2. Attach that loose chip, Solder and Flux to the bench, and fit the charged 40v Battery.
  3. Hold the Blank KeyCard and aim at the Helping Hands area. Use Attach Keycard.
  4. Close the drawer, empty your hands and aim at the soldering area.
  5. Complete Install New Chip. This uses one chip, 20 Solder, 20 Flux and battery power.
  6. Remove the resulting card. It now has a chip but still needs programming.

Step B — program the card

  1. Hold the card with its new chip and aim at the bench’s card reader.
  2. Use Insert Keycard.
  3. Keep the drawer closed, empty your hands and aim at the laptop.
  4. Complete Program Keycard. This uses additional battery power.
  5. Remove the programmed card from the reader. For the five normal room colours, it is now an inactive card.

Step C — activate it

Put the inactive card in your hands and choose Activate. Check its colour and Remaining Uses before you leave. Programming alone does not finish the process.

Chip installation and programming both use battery power. Bring a healthy, charged 40v Battery with enough charge for both steps rather than relying on a nearly empty one.

Card uses and repairing a used card

Card colourConfigured uses when activated
Yellow1
Green1
Blue1
Purple1
Red1

An opening consumes a use. When the last use is spent, the card is returned as a Malfunctioning KeyCard. Keep the body if you want to reuse it.

  1. Attach the malfunctioning card to the bench’s Helping Hands area.
  2. With a charged battery and the drawer closed, use the soldering area’s Remove Chip action.
  3. Recover the Blank KeyCard. The removed burnt chip is not a replacement working chip.
  4. Install a fresh colour chip, program the resulting card, remove it and activate it again.

Repairing a spent card adds a third powered operation, so allow extra battery charge for removing the burnt chip. Installation still needs a new chip plus 20 Solder and 20 Flux. Treat repair as making a new colour card from a reused body, not as a free recharge.

No Vehicle Zones

The areas surrounding DNA SAFESTORE rooms are No Vehicle Zones. These zones are visibly marked on the in-game map, and driving a vehicle into one triggers an audible warning.

Exactly 15 seconds: from that warning, you must drive the vehicle completely outside the zone. If it is still inside after 15 seconds, the engine will be completely ruined.

Park outside the marked boundary and approach on foot.

Opening a room and getting out with your loot

  1. Check that your card is active, matches the SAFESTORE colour and has uses remaining.
  2. Clear nearby threats and identify cover and an exit route before using it.
  3. Hold the card and use the building’s keycard interaction. Follow the insert/unlock prompts; inserting the card and completing the opening are separate interactions.
  4. Allow the door/opening sequence to finish. Do not assume another card is needed just because the animation takes a few seconds.
  5. Loot the room’s container and keep watching the outside approaches.
  6. Leave room in your inventory before the run. Equipment, weapons and medical items can be bulky.

These are shared world locations, not personal reward instances. Another player may already have visited. A room visit does not guarantee a fresh loot roll, and opening/closing the same crate is not a refill mechanic. The four-hour supply restock setting is not a promise that a room refills after four hours.

Rewards by room colour

The DNA system is active, and each room rolls randomized rewards from its own pool. A room can give you a useful selection without giving you every possible item. Do not expect a specific colour, a matching outfit, every weapon attachment or every item listed below on every visit.

How to read these lists: Quantities are configured selections from the room’s reward pool, not an unconditional promise that every listed item will appear or fit in the container. Gear comes as individual pieces, and separate ammunition rewards are assorted.

Red rooms and the separate endgame locked rooms

Red is the top DNA tier, but it is not the final reward destination. Red-room rewards include access cards for separate locked rooms on the server. Look for those access cards among your rewards and read their item descriptions for the destination they unlock.

Those separate rooms hold the endgame reward pools: Mega storage gear, the heaviest weapon calibres such as 8mm, .300 Win Mag, .338 and .50 BMG, Pro injectors and Tactical Surgical Kits.

A Red DNA card opens the Red SAFESTORE. The separate access cards found in the Red reward pool are what you need for the endgame locked rooms. They are different items, so keep the correct card for the destination you want. As with other loot, an access card is not guaranteed in every roll.

ATF warning — opening the crate can bring a fight

Prepare for ATF reinforcements

Opening a fresh DNA crate brings 3–5 armed ATF reinforcements. They spawn outside near the building and hunt the player who opened it. They are not friendly guards waiting politely at the door.

Get your loot, watch your exits and expect a fight on the way out.

This warning applies at every DNA colour, including Yellow. A lower loot tier does not mean an unguarded exit.

  • Arrive with a reliable weapon, spare ammunition, bandages and room for the rewards.
  • Bring a teammate to watch the approaches while you handle the card and crate.
  • Expect armed AI with a rifle and pistol using our ATF equipment loadout.
  • Get into cover before sorting loot. The response is tied to opening the crate, not merely walking up to the building.
  • The opener is the pursuit target, but teammates and other players can also draw enemy attention. Do not assume anyone nearby is safe.
  • Do not rely on closing the door or reopening the crate to reset the encounter.

All normal server PvE/PvP and building rules still apply. This guide does not declare a new PvP zone or override the rules for any location.

Before you leave for a room

  • Correct-colour, activated keycard with uses left
  • Working primary weapon, compatible magazines and spare ammunition
  • Bandages and appropriate medical supplies
  • Protection for contamination if your route requires it
  • Enough inventory space to take rewards home
  • Park vehicles outside the marked No Vehicle Zone and approach on foot
  • Room coordinates, cover and an exit route
  • A teammate watching outside if possible

For the personal-workbench route: Build the bench. Make the card. Plan your exit. Getting the door open is only part of the run.
