Guide // Navigation
Navigation & Travel

LBGroups
GPS

Set routes from your map, follow them on your GPS HUD and switch between drivable and road-only navigation while you travel.

Before You Start

Open the large map with M and make sure a GPS is equipped and active so the navigation system can track your location.

Set a Destination

01. Open your large map with M.
02. Find the marker you want to navigate to, or create a new marker.
03. Double right-click the marker to open the Edit Marker menu.
04. Select + Route in the top-right corner.
The route line appears on both the large map and the GPS HUD/minimap.

Driving Modes

Press J while moving to switch modes. The key can be rebound in your controls.

  • Drivable: builds a route across surfaces a vehicle can physically cross, including paved roads, dirt roads and gravel paths.
  • Road Only: prioritizes high-quality surfaces such as asphalt roads, highways, airfields and major bridges.

The on-screen notification confirms which mode is active.

Live Recalculation

Missed a turn or taken an unexpected shortcut? You do not need to reset the route. LBGroups GPS uses live pathfinding to recalculate from your new position and continue toward the destination.

Clear a Route

01. Open the large map with M.
02. Double right-click the active marker.
03. Select - Route in the top-right corner to remove the pathing line.

Quick Reference

M Open map · Double right-click marker · + Route start navigation · J switch driving mode · - Route clear navigation
