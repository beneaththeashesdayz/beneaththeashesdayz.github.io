Start here

These recipes are tailored to Beneath the Ashes Chernarus settings. Begin by crafting a pallet, then combine it with the listed materials to make a kit. Attach the extra materials needed for the finished object before placing it.

Important pallet rule. The pallet used to craft a kit is consumed. It does not count toward the pallets you attach afterward.

Enabled on Chernarus

Pallets, tables, cabinets, beds, boxes, special kits, barrel stoves, rain collectors, old wooden crates and most workbench recipes.

Kit types

Crafted kits need their building materials attached before placement. Ready-made boxed kits are already set up for their specific item.

Disabled functionality

BoomLay personal depots are disabled as a feature. This is separate from crafting availability and does not affect other storage systems on the server.

Disabled crafting. Sofa kits, coffee-machine crafting and paper-label crafting are disabled on Chernarus. A disabled crafting recipe does not establish whether a ready-made item is available through loot or traders.

Furniture cost breakdown

The initial kit and the materials attached afterward are separate steps. The first pallet is consumed when you craft the kit and does not count toward the attached pallets.

Tables

Initial kit: 1 pallet + 5 wooden planks.

Additional: 1, 2 or 3 attached pallets.

Total: Small 2 pallets + 5 planks; Medium 3 pallets + 5 planks; Large 4 pallets + 5 planks.

Cabinets

Initial kit: 1 pallet + 5 wooden planks.

Additional: 1, 2, 3 or 4 attached pallets.

Total: Shelf 2 pallets + 5 planks; Gun Cabinet 3 pallets + 5 planks; Medium 4 pallets + 5 planks; Large 5 pallets + 5 planks.

Beds

Initial kit: 1 pallet + 5 wooden planks.

Additional: 3 pallets for Small Bed or 4 pallets for Large Bed.

Total: Small 4 pallets + 5 planks; Large 5 pallets + 5 planks.

Boxes

Initial kit: 1 pallet + 4 wooden planks.

Additional: 1, 2, 3 or 4 Pallet Frames.

Total: 1 pallet + 4 planks + the selected frame count. Each frame costs 4 planks + 24 nails.

The shelf is designed for crate storage. Old Wooden Crates can be crafted separately for compatible cabinet slots.

Workbench — how to use it

Build a Workbench with the Special Kit recipe, then attach ingredients to the bench’s matching material slots. Hold the required tool and aim at the bench to choose a crafting action.

Attachments matter. Materials must be attached to the workbench, not merely carried in your backpack or placed in its general cargo.

Screwdriver recipes

Old Fridge, Repairbench, Small Spotlight, Desk Lamp and Floor Lamp.

Blowtorch recipes

Solar Panel and Firewood Storage. The blowtorch needs an attached gas canister with enough fuel.

Consumes 25% of a full Electronics Repair Kit; bring at least 25% remaining. The recipe scripts consume a quarter-kit even where an older online guide mentions a whole kit.

Some recipes require more material to be loaded than they consume. The recipe cards below list both amounts so the crafting option will appear reliably.

Coffee-machine crafting is disabled on Chernarus. The online recipe will not become available at the workbench.

Moving furniture and avoiding crafting problems

Hold a Screwdriver or Pliers and use the dismantle action on supported BoomLay furniture. Chernarus is configured to return building resources with the appropriate kit where that item supports it. This is not a refund of every material originally used to craft the kit.

For a greenhouse, stand inside and aim toward its middle to find the dismantle action. Empty furniture and remove stored valuables before dismantling it.

If a recipe or placement option is missing

  1. Check that the recipe is enabled in this guide.
  2. Make sure ingredients and tools are not ruined.
  3. Use loose nails and the correct item: full Pallet versus Pallet Frame, or Tarp/Fabric versus rags.
  4. Put ingredients into the named attachment slots.
  5. Check exact pallet counts and remove fabric from Special Kits intended for pallet furniture.
  6. Load the extra sheets or planks listed for the spotlight and lamp recipes.
  7. Hold the correct tool and check blowtorch fuel.
  8. Reposition a red placement preview to a valid area.
Server behavior. Doors close on server restart. Automatic timed closing is disabled. Sitting and sleeping interactions are enabled; this does not by itself establish any custom fatigue or healing benefit.

Availability and references

Not craftable through the enabled recipes: sofas, coffee machines, paper labels, indoor plants, paintings and carpets. Crafting being disabled does not establish whether a ready-made item is available through loot or traders.

BoomLay personal depots are a disabled feature, separate from the crafting recipes listed above. This refers to this mod’s depot feature, not other storage systems on the server.

These recipes are tailored to Chernarus’s saved settings and installed scripts as checked on October 27, 2026. Older online instructions can differ, particularly for Box Kits, Old Wooden Crates and workbench ingredient thresholds.

Original mod and crafting instructions by BoomLay; the installed DayZ 1.30 update is maintained by Vitryol.
