Crafting Guide

Build furniture, storage, lighting and a workshop for your base. These recipes follow the Beneath the Ashes Chernarus settings checked on October 27, 2026.

Start here

These recipes are tailored to Beneath the Ashes Chernarus settings. Begin by crafting a pallet, then combine it with the listed materials to make a kit. Attach the extra materials needed for the finished object before placing it.

Important pallet rule. The pallet used to craft a kit is consumed. It does not count toward the pallets you attach afterward.

Enabled on Chernarus

Pallets, tables, cabinets, beds, boxes, special kits, barrel stoves, rain collectors, old wooden crates and most workbench recipes.

Kit types

Crafted kits need their building materials attached before placement. Ready-made boxed kits are already set up for their specific item.

Disabled crafting. Sofa kits, coffee machines, paper labels and BoomLay personal depots are disabled on Chernarus. Do not spend materials following those online recipes.

Pallets, frames and crates

Wooden Pallet

Combine 10 Wooden Planks + 30 Nails. Produces 1 pallet. No additional tool is required.

Pallet Frame

Combine 4 Wooden Planks + 24 Nails. Produces 1 frame for the Box Kit. A frame is separate from a full pallet.

Old Wooden Crate

Combine 10 Wooden Planks + 24 Nails. Produces the BoomLay old crate, used with compatible furniture storage slots.

Dismantling a pallet

Hold a Hatchet, Splitting Axe/Wood Axe, Firefighter Axe, Pickaxe or Crowbar and use the dismantle action on the pallet.

Returns 6 Wooden Planks + 20 Nails on Chernarus. Crafting and dismantling loses materials, so dismantling is useful for salvaging a found pallet, not recovering its full crafting cost.

Tables, cabinets, beds and boxes

Tables and cabinets

Table Kit

Combine 1 Pallet + 5 Wooden Planks and choose the Table Kit recipe.

  • 1 pallet attached: Small Table
  • 2 pallets attached: Medium Table
  • 3 pallets attached: Large Table

Cabinet Kit

Combine 1 Pallet + 5 Wooden Planks and choose the Cabinet Kit recipe.

  • 1 pallet attached: Pallet Shelf
  • 2 pallets attached: Gun Cabinet
  • 3 pallets attached: Medium Cabinet
  • 4 pallets attached: Large Cabinet
Attached-pallet counts are extra. They are in addition to the pallet spent making the kit. A Small Table therefore requires 2 pallets total + 5 planks. Once the correct materials are attached, hold the kit, choose its placement action, position the preview and complete placement.

The shelf is designed for crate storage. Old Wooden Crates can be crafted separately for compatible cabinet slots.

Beds and storage boxes

Bed Kit

Combine 1 Pallet + 5 Wooden Planks and choose the Bed Kit recipe.

  • Attach 3 additional pallets for the Small Bed: 4 pallets total + 5 planks.
  • Attach 4 additional pallets for the Large Bed: 5 pallets total + 5 planks.

Box Kit

Combine 1 Pallet + 4 Wooden Planks and choose the Box Kit recipe. Attach 1, 2, 3 or 4 Pallet Frames to select box sizes 1 through 4.

Use frames, not additional full pallets, in the Box Kit frame slots. Each frame costs 4 planks + 24 nails; the smallest box needs 1 pallet, 4 planks for the kit and 1 separately crafted frame.

Labels and sofas. Pallet-box labeling is enabled, but crafting new paper labels is disabled. Existing suitable labels can still be used. Crafting the Sofa Kit is disabled on Chernarus.

Special Kit

Combine 1 Pallet + 5 Wooden Planks and choose Special Item Kit. Then attach the materials for the object you want.

Trash Can

Attach 2 additional pallets with no fabric attached. Total: 3 pallets + 5 planks. If every pallet is crafted from raw materials: 35 planks + 90 nails.

Workbench

Attach 3 additional pallets with no fabric attached. Total: 4 pallets + 5 planks.

Greenhouse

Attach 5 additional pallets with no fabric attached. Total: 6 pallets + 5 planks.

Log Storage Place

Attach 4 Tarp/Fabric units with no pallets attached. Total: 1 pallet + 5 planks + 4 Tarp/Fabric units.

Use one exact material combination. Mixing fabric and pallets in a Special Kit will not select these recipes. Trash cans are for disposal: their contents are configured to be cleared when the server loads after a restart. Keep valuables in proper storage.

Barrel stove and rain collector

Barrel Stove

  1. Empty a barrel completely.
  2. Combine the empty barrel with a Hacksaw to make the Stove Kit.
  3. Attach 10 Metal Sheets to the kit’s metal-sheet slot.
  4. Place the kit to build the stove.

The barrel is consumed by the recipe; the hacksaw is retained. The 10 sheets are additional building materials required for placement.

Rain Collector

  1. Combine 1 Pallet + 4 Metal Sheets to make the Rain Collector Kit.
  2. Attach 4 Tarp/Fabric units to its material slot.
  3. Place the collector outside where it can collect precipitation.

Fabric means the game’s Tarp/Fabric item, not rags. The installed updated mod supports rain and snow collection; keep the collector outdoors with clear exposure to precipitation.

Workbench — how to use it

Build a Workbench with the Special Kit recipe, then attach ingredients to the bench’s matching material slots. Hold the required tool and aim at the bench to choose a crafting action.

Attachments matter. Materials must be attached to the workbench, not merely carried in your backpack or placed in its general cargo.

Screwdriver recipes

Old Fridge, Repairbench, Small Spotlight, Desk Lamp and Floor Lamp.

Blowtorch recipes

Solar Panel and Firewood Storage. The blowtorch needs an attached gas canister with enough fuel.

Recipes using an Electronics Repair Kit consume 25 percentage points of its supply. Bring a kit with at least 25% remaining. The recipe scripts consume a quarter-kit even where an older online guide mentions a whole kit.

Some recipes require more material to be loaded than they consume. The recipe cards below list both amounts so the crafting option will appear reliably.

Coffee-machine crafting is disabled on Chernarus. The online recipe will not become available at the workbench.

Workbench recipes

Fridge, repairbench and spotlight

Old Fridge — hold a Screwdriver

Load 10 Metal Sheets + 1 Metal Wire + an Electronics Repair Kit. Consumes 10 sheets, 1 wire and 25% of the repair kit. Produces an Old Fridge Kit.

Repairbench — hold a Screwdriver

Load 10 Metal Sheets + 10 Wooden Planks + 30 Nails. Consumes those materials and produces a Repairbench Kit.

Small Spotlight — hold a Screwdriver

Load at least 2 Metal Sheets + 1 Metal Wire + an Electronics Repair Kit. Consumes 1 sheet, 1 wire and 25% of the repair kit. The spare sheet remains afterward.

Using the finished equipment

Old Fridge

Configured to work without electricity and has a food-preservation factor of 3.

Repairbench

Attach suitable repair kits to the repairbench, hold the damaged item and use its repair action. Repairing to pristine is enabled for supported items; normal item and repair-kit compatibility still applies.

Small Spotlight

Uses a 9V battery for operation.

Lamps, solar panel and firewood storage

Desk Lamp — hold a Screwdriver

Load at least 2 Metal Sheets + 4 Wooden Planks + an Electronics Repair Kit. Consumes 1 sheet, 3 planks and 25% of the repair kit. The extra sheet and plank remain afterward.

Floor Lamp — hold a Screwdriver

Load 1 Metal Pipe + 1 Metal Wire + at least 3 Wooden Planks + an Electronics Repair Kit. Consumes 1 pipe, 1 wire, 2 planks and 25% of the repair kit. The extra plank remains afterward.

Solar Panel — hold a fueled Blowtorch

Load 10 Metal Sheets + 1 Metal Wire + an Electronics Repair Kit. Consumes 10 sheets, 1 wire and 25% of the repair kit. Produces a Solar Panel Kit.

Firewood Storage — hold a fueled Blowtorch

Load 4 Metal Sheets. Consumes all 4 sheets and produces a Firewood Storage Kit.

For operation, the solar panel charges a truck battery during daylight and must be placed outside, away from a roof. The Floor Lamp needs external power and its dusk sensor limits it to nighttime operation.

Moving furniture and avoiding crafting problems

Hold a Screwdriver or Pliers and use the dismantle action on supported BoomLay furniture. Chernarus is configured to return building resources with the appropriate kit where that item supports it. This is not a refund of every material originally used to craft the kit.

For a greenhouse, stand inside and aim toward its middle to find the dismantle action. Empty furniture and remove stored valuables before dismantling it.

If a recipe or placement option is missing

  1. Check that the recipe is enabled in this guide.
  2. Make sure ingredients and tools are not ruined.
  3. Use loose nails and the correct item: full Pallet versus Pallet Frame, or Tarp/Fabric versus rags.
  4. Put ingredients into the named attachment slots.
  5. Check exact pallet counts and remove fabric from Special Kits intended for pallet furniture.
  6. Load the extra sheets or planks listed for the spotlight and lamp recipes.
  7. Hold the correct tool and check blowtorch fuel.
  8. Reposition a red placement preview to a valid area.
Server behavior. Doors close on server restart. Automatic timed closing is disabled. Sitting and sleeping interactions are enabled; this does not by itself establish any custom fatigue or healing benefit.

Availability and references

Not craftable through the enabled recipes: sofas, coffee machines, paper labels, indoor plants, paintings and carpets. Crafting being disabled does not establish whether a ready-made item is available through loot or traders.

BoomLay personal depots are disabled. This refers to this mod’s depot feature, not other storage systems on the server.

These recipes are tailored to Chernarus’s saved settings and installed scripts as checked on October 27, 2026. Older online instructions can differ, particularly for Box Kits, Old Wooden Crates and workbench ingredient thresholds.
