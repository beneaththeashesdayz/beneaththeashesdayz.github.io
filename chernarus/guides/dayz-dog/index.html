Dog

A loyal companion makes the long roads of Chernarus feel a little less lonely. Adopt your dog at one of BTA’s two public doghouses using Zombie Notes, choose from 35 breed and appearance options, and give your four-legged survivor a place to rest at home.

Quick Start — Getting Your First Dog

  1. Carry enough physical Zombie Notes in your character’s inventory. You can use multiple stacks. Notes in a bank account, vehicle, or ground container do not count toward adoption.
  2. Approach the public doghouse and select Adopt a dog. Stay within about three metres while choosing and confirming.
  3. Choose your dog and check the displayed price. Every option shows its cost, and the confirmation button reads Adopt for [price] Zombie Notes.
  4. Confirm the adoption. A successful purchase deducts the exact price and assigns your chosen dog to you. Opening or cancelling the menu does not charge you.
  5. Give your new dog the Follow command before you leave. Newly adopted dogs begin on Stay here.

No bone is needed to adopt. BTA uses a paid, choose-your-dog menu rather than the original mod’s bone-based setup. There is no random adoption button, and player-built kennels cannot offer adoptions. If you do not have enough notes, the purchase is rejected without charging you.

Commands & Controls

Check Options → Controls → DayZ Dog to view or change your bindings. The keys below are the mod defaults; your existing bindings may differ.

Commands and default keybindings
CommandDefault keyWhat it does
WhistleNumpad +Calls your active dog to your position and puts it into Follow. Use this if it gets stuck or falls behind.
FollowNumpad /Brings your dog along with you. It can respond to infected threatening you.
PatrolNumpad *Lets your dog engage nearby infected.
Stay hereNumpad -Keeps your dog in place. Use this before treating an injury.
Toggle dog emotesNumpad 9Toggles the mod's emote command feature.

You can also use the available interaction prompts while looking at your dog. Allow a moment between commands. Whistle while on foot, with enough room for the dog to appear beside you.

Map-based waypoint missions are disabled on BTA Chernarus. Use Follow, Patrol, and Stay here for everyday commands.

Keep control around other survivors. Dog attacks against players are not disabled on Chernarus. Follow the server’s combat rules. The mod’s automatic punishment for killing a dog is also disabled; community rules and staff decisions still apply.

Naming & Caring for Your Dog

Look at your dog and select Dog Stats to check its health and recorded activity. To give it a name, use the name field and naming button in the management screen.

Feeding, Equipment & Treating Injuries

Feeding your dog

Hold a real bone or supported meat in your hands, look at your dog and use Feed Dog. Supported meat includes deer, cow, pig, goat, boar, bear and sheep steaks, rabbit legs, and chicken breasts.

Before feeding, empty the dog’s mouth slot and remove its dog gas mask. The Feed Dog action will not appear while either slot is occupied. Let the eating action finish before giving another command.

A bone toy is not food. The dog bowl and bone toy are accessories; buying a bowl does not replace feeding your dog with a supported item.

Fetch and accessories

For a game of fetch, keep your dog nearby, remove its gas mask and mouth attachment, and throw the ordinary dog bone toy. Start on clear, open ground.

Equipment

Fit compatible collars, vests, and dog gas masks through the inventory attachment slots. Check your dog’s health after combat: equipment does not make it invulnerable.

Treating an injury

  1. Command your dog to Stay here.
  2. Hold a usable bandage and crouch beside it.
  3. Look at the wounded dog and complete Bandage dog's wound.

The treatment prompt appears only when the dog is wounded and staying in place. Dogs can die from their injuries. Carry bandages and pull your companion out of dangerous fights.

Kennels & Dog Storage

Buy a kennel kit from Rowan or Hazel and deploy it at your base. Kennel kits do not spawn in the loot economy.

To store your dog, approach an empty deployed kennel with your dog assigned to you, look at the kennel, and choose Leave dog in kennel. To retrieve it, return with a free dog slot and choose Take dog from kennel. Only the stored dog’s owner can retrieve it.

Each kennel stores one dog. Retrieving your stored dog does not cost another adoption payment. Kennels provide storage and retrieval only; they never offer a menu to adopt a new dog.

Keep your kennel protected and accessible. Use the kennel interaction to store your dog. Telling it to Stay beside a kennel does not store it.

Traveling With Your Dog

Set your dog to Follow before entering a vehicle. The mod temporarily stores the following dog and brings it back when you get out. A dog left on Stay or Patrol does not use this Follow travel behaviour.

After reconnecting, give your active dog time to load. It can return at its last saved location. If it is away from you, use Whistle while on foot. If it still does not return, contact staff before paying for a replacement.

Important Ownership Information

Only one dog can be active per character. You cannot adopt or retrieve another dog while your character’s dog slot is occupied.

Character death clears your active dog ownership. The adoption payment does not protect an active dog from loss. When leaving dogs at home, store them in a kennel using the proper action.

Troubleshooting

No Adopt a dog prompt
Check that you are at one of the two public houses, close enough to interact, and do not already have a dog assigned to your character. Your own kennel cannot offer adoptions.
Not enough Zombie Notes
Carry enough physical notes in your character's inventory. Notes in nearby storage or a bank balance do not pay for dog adoption.
New dog stays behind
Newly adopted dogs start on Stay. Use Follow or Whistle.
Dog will not eat
Hold supported food, remove its gas mask and mouth attachment, and wait for any current eating action to finish.
No bandaging prompt
The dog must be wounded and on Stay. Hold a usable bandage and crouch beside it.
Cannot retrieve a kennel dog
Make sure it is your stored dog and your character's dog slot is free. Move close and aim at the kennel's interaction point.
Dog missing after reconnect
Wait for loading, try Whistle on foot, and ask staff for help if it remains missing. Include your location, dog choice and what happened before it disappeared.
