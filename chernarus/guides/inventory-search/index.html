Opening Inventory Search

O

Press O in-game to open Inventory Search and start looking through your stored items.

Keybinding: Change the key under Controls → BTA Inventory Finder. Existing test profiles may still have F7 until the key is rebound.

Searching Your Inventory

  1. Step 01

    Search for an Item

    Search by item name, classname, or container location.

  2. Step 02

    Understand the Results

    Contents appear indented beneath their containers, so you can see what is stored inside them.

  3. Step 03

    Identify Item Locations

    The location column shows where each item is stored. Select an item to see its full location.

  4. Step 04

    Browse Longer Lists

    Scroll through the results when the list is longer than the visible area.

Dropping Items

Double-left-click an item to drop it at your feet.

Warning — Dropping Containers

Dropping a container also drops everything stored inside it. Double-check your selection before dropping anything!
