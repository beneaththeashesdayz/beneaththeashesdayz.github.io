Vehicles & Charging

Tesla Cybertruck
Charging

Need to top up your truck? Line it up at a Supercharger, then follow these steps to charge it.

Before You Start

Your Cybertruck needs its Cybertruck battery installed. You also need a deployed Tesla Supercharger. If you’re placing your own, deploy it using the Supercharger Kit.

Quick Version

Select Show Guide, park in the hologram, look at the charger, then select Charge.

How to Charge

01 · Show the parking guide

Approach the Supercharger and select Show Guide to display a hologram showing where to park.

02 · Position your Cybertruck

Park inside the hologram, matching the truck’s position and direction to the guide.

03 · Find the charging option

Exit your Cybertruck and look at the Supercharger. If the truck is positioned correctly, the Charge option will appear.

04 · Start charging

Select Charge to begin charging the battery. The option will change to Stop Charging while charging is active.

05 · Let the battery charge

Leave your Cybertruck parked until charging finishes automatically, or select Stop Charging to finish early.

06 · Hide the parking guide

Select Hide Guide to remove the hologram when you’re finished.

Charging Times

The Supercharger adds 5 energy units every second. A healthy battery holds 5,000 units, so charging takes approximately:

Charge neededTime
0% → 100%16 minutes 40 seconds
50% → 100%8 minutes 20 seconds
20% → 80%10 minutes

That’s roughly 1% every 10 seconds, or 6% per minute. Charging stops automatically when full.

Not Getting a Charging Prompt?

Charging option does not appear

If Show Guide works but Charge does not appear, reposition the truck inside the hologram and match its direction. Then exit and look directly at the Supercharger again.

It says Multiple Vehicles Detected

Move the other Cybertruck away from the station. The charger needs to detect one truck at a time.

Charging starts, then stops

Check that the Cybertruck battery is installed and is not already full. Charging also stops if the truck moves out of the detection area.

The parking guide does not appear

Tell staff which station you’re using. In-game testing confirmed that a charger deployed from its kit worked correctly; an Object Builder placement did not behave the same way.

Quick Reference

Show Guide, park in the hologram, then select Charge.

Still stuck? Send staff a screenshot of the truck’s position beside the charger and tell them which action you see.
