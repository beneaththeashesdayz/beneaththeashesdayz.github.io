Survival & Resources

Enhanced
Mining

There is more beneath Chernarus than dirt and stone. Create your own mine, travel underground and search for ores, minerals and gemstones—but prepare before you start digging.

What You’ll Need

Before starting a mine, bring:

  • A shovel
  • A pickaxe or another valid mining tool
  • Warm clothing
  • Food and water
  • A reliable light source
  • Wooden logs if you plan to expand or dig vertically
  • Enough inventory space for whatever you find
A shovel creates your entrance. Once underground, use a pickaxe or another supported mining tool to break through mine walls and expand your tunnels.

Before You Dig

Mining can be extremely rewarding, but heading underground unprepared can leave you cold, lost or unable to climb back out.

Plan your route. Bring warmth, light, supplies and enough logs to reinforce the path before digging deeper.

Creating a Mine Entrance

To begin:

  1. Place a shovel in your hands.
  2. Find a suitable dirt or forest surface.
  3. Look toward the ground and move around until the option to create a mine entrance appears.
  4. Dig the entrance.
  5. Interact with the completed entrance to enter the mine.

If you cannot create an entrance where you’re standing, move to another area and try again. Entrances need enough space around them and cannot be placed too close to another entrance. Do not assume every patch of dirt will work.

Entering Your Mine

Approach your completed entrance and use the interaction prompt to enter. You’ll be transported into the underground mining system.

Your entrance leads to a starting junction, which begins reinforced. From there, the digging begins.

How Mining Works

Underground mines are built from connected junctions. Each junction can potentially expand in six directions:

  • Forward
  • Backward
  • Left
  • Right
  • Up
  • Down

Use your pickaxe or another valid mining tool on the walls, floor or ceiling to open new sections. Each new section expands your mine and gives you another opportunity to uncover resources.

Reinforcing Your Mine

As you move farther from your starting point, tunnels become less stable. Wooden logs can be attached to mine sections to reinforce them.

Bring logs underground and attach them to the section you want to reinforce. Reinforcement is especially important if you plan to mine vertically.

WARNING: REINFORCE BEFORE DIGGING DOWN.
Do not begin digging downward unless you are prepared to reinforce your route. Vertical travel may require reinforcement in order for you to safely get back up.

Getting excited about shiny rocks is understandable. Becoming a permanent underground resident is less ideal.

Mining Up and Down

Mining vertically can open entirely new layers of your mine, but it carries more risk than expanding sideways.

  • Make sure you have enough wooden logs.
  • Reinforce the section properly.
  • Keep track of the route back to your entrance.
  • Carry enough supplies in case the trip takes longer than expected.

If you’re learning the system, expanding horizontally first is the safer option.

What Can You Find?

The Enhanced Mining system includes a variety of resources. Not every area will produce the same materials, so exploration matters.

Metals
Iron · Copper · Aluminium · Lead · Zinc · Tin · Silver · Gold
Minerals & Materials
Coal · Saltpeter · Sulfur · Salt Crystals · Clay · Quartz
Gemstones
Amethyst · Topaz · Ruby · Sapphire · Diamond

Ore Hotspots

Resource distribution is not necessarily equal across Chernarus. Certain areas may contain stronger concentrations of particular ores or gemstones, and some regions may be especially valuable while others produce fewer rare resources.

You do not need to find a hotspot in order to mine, but where you create your mine can influence what you discover. Explore and discover productive mining areas rather than relying on exact coordinates.

The Mines Are Cold

Do not underestimate the environment underground. Mines are intentionally cold, so wear warm clothing and pay attention to your temperature while you’re below ground.

WARNING: MINES ARE COLD.
Bring warm clothing, food, water and a reliable light source. Treat a long mining trip like any other dangerous expedition.

Lighting

Bring your own light source. Mining underground without reliable lighting makes navigation much more difficult and can make it easy to lose track of your route.

Consider carrying a backup light source if you’re planning a long trip.

Don’t Get Lost

Player-created mines can become surprisingly large. As you expand:

  • Pay attention to intersections.
  • Keep a mental note of your route.
  • Avoid opening unnecessary branches.
  • Consider creating a consistent pattern when expanding your tunnels.
  • Remember which direction leads back to your entrance.

The deeper the mine becomes, the easier it is to turn a carefully planned operation into an underground maze.

Mine Entrances Are Important

Choose your mine location carefully. Mine entrances are persistent structures and generally cannot simply be dismantled by a player once placed.

Do not place one randomly just to test the system. Pick an area you are comfortable returning to. If an entrance has been placed somewhere problematic, contact Beneath the Ashes staff for assistance.

Beginner Mining Checklist

  • Shovel
  • Pickaxe or mining tool
  • Warm clothing
  • Light source
  • Food
  • Water
  • Wooden logs for reinforcement
  • Free inventory space

For your first mine, stay relatively close to your entrance until you understand how the tunnels and reinforcement system work.

Important

The Enhanced Mining system is configurable by the server. This guide focuses on how players use the system, so technical values such as exact ore percentages, maximum depth, entrance spacing or reinforcement counts should not be treated as permanent Beneath the Ashes rules unless staff confirms them from the current server configuration.
