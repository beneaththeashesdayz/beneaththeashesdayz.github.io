Quick Start — Getting Your First Horse

  1. Make sure you have no active horse assigned to your character. Stable your current horse before adopting another.
  2. Carry enough physical Zombie Notes in your character’s inventory for the horse you want. A bank balance or notes left in nearby storage cannot pay for adoption.
  3. Approach Rowan or Hazel directly and open the trader’s menu. Browse Horse Adoptions - Saddle and Bridle Included, then choose your horse and its colour or appearance variant.
  4. Check the displayed price, set the quantity to one horse, and complete the purchase. Stay close to the trader and keep the nearby horse collection area clear.
  5. Collect your chosen horse from the trader’s delivery point. It is registered to you with a basic saddle and bridle already fitted. You do not need to bring either item for a trader adoption. Use the horse’s riding prompt to mount.

Horses: speak to the trader. Dogs: use the public doghouse. Horse options are inside Rowan’s or Hazel’s trader menu. The doghouse beside them is for dog adoption and does not sell horses. Prefer the wild route? See finding and claiming wild horses.

Finding & Claiming Wild Horses

Wild-horse claiming is still available alongside trader adoption. This route uses your own equipment. Choose an unowned wild horse and keep your character’s active horse slot free.

  1. Gather a saddle and bridle. Unlike a trader adoption, a wild horse does not arrive with these fitted for you.
  2. If needed, hold an apple and use the horse-bait interaction to lure a horse before you have a saddle.
  3. Hold the saddle in your hands, approach the unowned horse, and use the attach action to claim it.
  4. Attach the bridle, then use the horse’s interaction prompt to mount.

An apple can also call your owned horse. Hold an apple and use the call interaction while on foot. The current call range is 200 metres, and the horse needs clear space to appear near you. The call does not retrieve a horse stored in a stable.

Riding Controls & Stamina

Start slowly and give yourself room to learn the turns. These are the usual controls; existing keybindings may differ. The mod’s speed bindings are under Options → Controls → DayZ Horse.

Move & steerWASDMove forward, turn, and move backward.
Change speedMouse wheel up / downIncrease or decrease your riding speed.
JumpSpaceJump while riding.
DismountInteraction promptUse the on-screen action to get off your horse.

Horses have their own stamina. Faster riding drains it. Slow down or stop to let your horse recover before asking for more speed.

Watch the route

Trees, fences, and other obstacles can throw you off in a collision. Ease off the speed when the route gets tight.

Horse Equipment & Accessories

A saddle and bridle are required for riding. Trader adoptions include a basic set already attached. Keep both fitted when you ride; for wild claiming, bring your own.

Horse bags provide additional storage. They are separate accessories and are not part of the adoption’s included saddle-and-bridle set.

Building a Stable

A stable gives you a proper place to store horses between journeys. Combine 1 rope + 2 short sticks, select Craft Stable Kit, and place the kit in a suitable location.

Stage 1

  • 15 wooden logs
  • 2 planks
  • 20 nails

Stage 2

  • 20 planks
  • 80 nails

Stage 3

  • 20 planks
  • 60 nails

Materials total: 15 logs, 42 planks, and 160 nails, plus the rope and sticks for the kit.

Use the appropriate construction tool and follow the build prompts through all three stages. The stable must be fully finished before you can use its horse storage.

Storing & Retrieving Horses

Putting your horse in the stable

  1. Fully finish the stable.
  2. Bring your claimed horse nearby and dismount.
  3. Approach an empty horse bay.
  4. Aim at the bay’s horse-slot area and select the put horse into stable action.

Three horse slots: A completed craftable stable has three available horse slots. If the prompt does not appear, adjust your position and aim around the empty bay.

Parking is not stable storage

Simply parking a horse inside the stable does not store it. Complete the actual put horse into stable interaction before treating the horse as stored.

Taking your horse out

Return with no active horse assigned to your character, aim at the occupied horse slot, and select the take horse from stable action. Keep the space beside the stable clear for the horse.

Horse Ownership & Important Warnings

One active horse per character

You cannot adopt another horse or retrieve a stabled horse while you already have an active horse. Stable your current horse first. This limit applies to adopted and wild-claimed horses alike.

Character death clears active horse ownership

When your character dies, the active horse is released as a wild horse and its ownership is cleared. Buying a horse does not protect it from this loss of ownership.

After disconnecting or reconnecting

The horse system saves your active horse’s position, health, and inventory when you disconnect and restores it when you reconnect. Allow time for it to load at the saved position. If it is nearby, use an apple to call it into clear space; the call range is 200 metres.

If the horse does not return, contact staff before adopting or claiming a replacement. Include where you were and what happened before it disappeared.

Keep your stable protected and accessible. Store horses through the proper interaction and follow the official Chernarus server rules. Riding or stable access does not grant permission to take another survivor’s horse.

Troubleshooting

Looking for a horse at the doghouse?
Speak to Rowan or Hazel instead. Open the trader menu and browse Horse Adoptions - Saddle and Bridle Included. The public doghouse is only for dog adoption.
Not enough Zombie Notes?
Carry the payment physically on your character. If the trader does not count your notes, move them out of nested containers into your character’s inventory.
Adoption says you already have a horse?
Properly stable your current horse before buying another. A parked horse still occupies your active horse slot.
Horse delivery area occupied?
Clear the trader’s collection point, stay near Rowan or Hazel, and try again. The delivery checks reject the adoption without taking payment when the area is blocked.
No riding prompt?
Check that a saddle and bridle are fitted. Put held items away, make sure nobody is already riding, and leave room beside the horse to mount.
Horse slowing down?
Check its stamina. Slow down or stop to let it recover.
No stable-storage prompt?
Confirm construction is complete, you are dismounted, and the bay is empty. Adjust your position and aim at the horse-slot area.
Cannot retrieve a horse?
Check whether you already have an active horse. Store it first, then aim at the occupied stable slot.
Horse missing after reconnect?
Give it time to load, check the last saved location, and try the apple call if it is within 200 metres. Contact staff if it still does not return.

Report problems to staff with a screenshot and a brief description of what happened. Include the trader or stable location, your horse choice, and the action you tried.
