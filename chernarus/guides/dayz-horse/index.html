DayZ
Horse

🐴 BTA Player Guide — DayZ Horse. Learn how to claim, ride, stable and retrieve your horse across Chernarus.

Getting Your Horse

Find a horse and gather a saddle and bridle. Hold the saddle in your hands, approach the horse, and use the attach action to claim it. Attach the bridle, then use the horse’s interaction prompt to mount.

🍎 Apple lure: An apple can lure a horse before you have a saddle. You can also use an apple to call your owned horse.

Riding Controls

  • WASD: Move and steer, including moving backward.
  • Mouse wheel: Change riding speed.
  • Space: Jump.
  • Use the on-screen interaction prompt to dismount.

Horses have their own stamina. Faster riding drains it, so slow down or stop to let your horse recover.

⚠️ Watch the route: Be careful around trees, fences, and other obstacles. Collisions can throw you off!

Equipment and Storage

  • Saddle + bridle: Required for riding.
  • Horse bags: Provide additional storage.

Building a Stable

Combine 1 rope + 2 short sticks, select Craft Stable Kit, and place the kit in a suitable location.

Stage 1

15 wooden logs, 2 planks, 20 nails.

Stage 2

20 planks, 80 nails.

Stage 3

20 planks, 60 nails.

📦 Materials total: 15 logs, 42 planks, and 160 nails, plus the rope and sticks for the kit.

Use the appropriate construction tool and follow the build prompts.

Putting Your Horse in the Stable

  1. Fully finish the stable.
  2. Bring your claimed horse nearby and dismount.
  3. Approach an empty horse bay.
  4. Aim at the bay’s horse-slot area and select the put horse into stable action.
Three horse slots: A stable has three available horse slots.
Important: Simply parking your horse inside does not complete the storage action.

If the prompt doesn’t appear, adjust your position and aim around the empty bay.

Taking Your Horse Out

Aim at the occupied horse slot and select the take horse from stable action.

Active horse limit: You cannot retrieve another horse while you already have an active horse assigned to you.

If Something Isn’t Working

  • No riding prompt? Check the saddle and bridle.
  • Horse slowing down? Check its stamina.
  • No stable-storage prompt? Confirm construction is complete, you’re dismounted, and the bay is empty.
  • Can’t retrieve a horse? Check whether you already have an active horse.
Please report problems to staff with a screenshot and a brief description of what happened.
