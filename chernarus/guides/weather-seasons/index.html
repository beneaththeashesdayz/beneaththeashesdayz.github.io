Weather &
Seasons

Beneath the Ashes uses a dynamic weather and seasonal system. Conditions, temperatures and seasons shift automatically, so checking the forecast can help you prepare before heading out.

Main HUD Weather Display

Your current weather information is always visible on the main HUD.

  • In-game time
  • Current temperature in Celsius
  • Current season

Example: 01:47 | -6°C | Winter

The temperature shown reflects current in-game conditions, so check it regularly when deciding what clothing or supplies to take.

Seasonal Cycle

There are four seasons, and each lasts for 7 real-world days:

Spring → Summer → Fall → Winter

When a season ends, the server automatically advances to the next one. Each season can bring different temperatures and weather conditions.

Checking the Forecast

Press M, then select Weather. The Weather Forecast panel appears on the left side of your screen.

The forecast shows current and upcoming conditions, including:

  • Temperature
  • Weather type and precipitation
  • Precipitation intensity or chance
  • Wind conditions and speed
  • Fog
  • Cloud coverage
  • Forecast confidence

Forecast periods are represented by icons and expected temperatures. Select a period for more detailed information.

Example Forecast

A winter forecast may show:

  • Snow
  • Temperature: -6°C
  • Precipitation: Heavy
  • Wind: Gale, 15 m/s
  • Fog: 0%
  • Cloud Cover: 75%
  • Confidence: 71%
That forecast is your warning that wandering into the wilderness in a hoodie and optimism may not be the strongest survival strategy.

Use the Forecast to Prepare

Before a long trip, hunting expedition, loot run or mountain journey, check the forecast. Watch your temperature, dress for the current season and use the forecast to see what conditions are moving in.
