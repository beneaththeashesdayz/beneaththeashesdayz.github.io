Research & Exploration

Locked Research
Facilities

Research Access Keycards open the doors to some of the rarest loot opportunities in Beneath the Ashes.

How It Works

01 · Get a keycard

Research Access Keycards can be found in Hacked Crates.

02 · Find a facility

Use your keycard to identify and reach one of the secured research facilities hidden across Chernarus.

03 · Unlock the door

Keycards are single-use. Once accepted by the entrance, the card is consumed.

04 · Search the facility

Explore every room and check every crate. Each facility contains multiple loot containers.

Survivor Tips

  • Bring a group—more eyes means more security.
  • Clear the surrounding area before unlocking the entrance.
  • Empty your inventory before entering so you have room for rewards.
  • Every keycard is valuable, and no two runs are guaranteed to be the same.
High risk, high reward. Bring ammunition, medical supplies, food, water and enough inventory space.

Possible Rewards

Rare MMG GearRetextured gear in rare colors, including tops, pants, bags, belts, helmets and attachments.
Paragon CollectablesHigh-tier collectibles that can be sold to Kaito at Cinder Market for a strong payout.
Bank Robbery SledgehammerA rare chance to find the tool needed for a major bank robbery opportunity.
