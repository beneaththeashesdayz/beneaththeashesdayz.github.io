Locked Research
Facilities

The Ember Research Institute and its sister facilities are the endgame destination beyond the coloured DNA SAFESTORE rooms. Recover the right credential, reach its matching facility and see what the Ashes have kept hidden.

What These Facilities Are

Locked research facilities are separate endgame locations reached through the Red DNA progression. They are not opened by the Red DNA card itself. The Red card gets you into the Red SAFESTORE; a recovered facility credential is the separate item that opens its matching research facility.

How To Obtain Access

01 · Build the DNA bench

Gather the parts and supplies needed for the DNA Electronics Workbench.

02 · Make a Red DNA card

Use the bench to craft, program and activate a Red DNA keycard. The DNA guide walks through the full process.

03 · Open the Red SAFESTORE

Take the active Red DNA card to a Red SAFESTORE. That card opens the Red room only; it does not directly unlock a research facility.

04 · Search the Red rewards

A recovered facility credential may appear in the Red room reward pool. It is random loot, not a guaranteed drop, and it is not crafted at the DNA bench.

05 · Visit the matching facility

Read the credential’s item description, then take it to the research facility named there. Each facility credential has one use.

Survivor Tips

  • Check the area before you commit to the door or the Red SAFESTORE.
  • Bring a reliable weapon, spare ammunition, medical supplies, food and water.
  • Leave room to carry bulky storage rewards home.
  • Read each credential description carefully; DNA colours and facility clearance colours are separate systems.
  • Plan your route out before you open a container.
High risk, high reward. The Red room is the route to a possible credential, but no single opening is guaranteed to provide one.

⚠️ ATF Reinforcements

Unlocking a research facility is a two-stage danger. The door’s normal unlock countdown runs first. Once the facility door actually opens, an 8-second reinforcement delay begins.

  1. After the delay, 5–7 armed ATF AI spawn around the facility and actively hunt the player who unlocked it.
  2. Approaching the facility or swiping the card alone does not immediately trigger the ATF wave.
  3. The response triggers once per successful facility opening, not once for every loot container opened inside.

This applies to Fort Greenville, Fort Kamensk, Tower Hill and Fort Tisy. These reinforcements are additional to the infected already present.

Plan ahead: Bring sufficient ammunition and medical supplies, bring a group if possible, and plan an escape route before opening the facility.

Each locked-room reward roll can draw from the reward categories below. The selection is randomized, and no container is guaranteed to provide every category.

Assorted AmmunitionAmmo from those high-caliber pools can appear, but it may not match a weapon found alongside it.
Mega MMG GearMega-storage clothing and belts, pouches and bags can appear, including Welliton colour variants. These are individual gear pieces, not guaranteed matching outfits. Standard MMG appears earlier in DNA progression; Half Mega appears in Purple and Red.
My Delta Force MedicalAdvanced Medical Kit, Tactical Surgical Kit and randomized Pro injectors. The kits are not advertised as fully stocked.
Paragon CollectablesRandom selections from the Paragon reward pool. A particular collectible, rarity, trader payout or sale price is not guaranteed.
BallerZ TeddiesA random teddy from the available colour selection. These are not BallerZ armour or clothing.
BTA XLarge WalletA 100-slot wallet with a 30% chance per locked-room reward roll.
Custom-Skinned BTA BarrelA 900-slot barrel with a 45% chance per locked-room reward roll, with at most one barrel from that selection per roll.
SledgehammerA rare bonus at approximately 1% per reward roll. Finding one does not by itself guarantee access to or completion of a bank robbery.

Preparation & Security Warnings

DNA crate warning: opening a DNA crate can call in 3–5 ATF AI who hunt the player responsible for opening it. This matters when you are searching the Red room for a possible facility credential.

Clear the surrounding area before opening a DNA crate, keep cover and an exit route in mind, and expect pressure on the way out. This warning is tied to opening the DNA crate; do not assume that opening the separate research-facility door triggers the same ATF response.

  • Bring ammunition, medical supplies, food and water.
  • Facility opening: bring a group if possible and plan your escape route before unlocking; the ATF wave arrives after the door opens.
  • Check the area before entry and avoid sorting your rewards in the open.
  • Plan how you will transport bulky items such as a Mega bag, wallet or barrel.
  • Do not invent a shortcut: the Red DNA card and recovered facility credential are separate items.
