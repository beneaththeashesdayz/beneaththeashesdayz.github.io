Survivor Field Guide // Chernarus

Geb's
Plants

There’s more to surviving the apocalypse than finding a can of beans and hoping it has not developed opinions.

Geb’s Plants brings 26 different crops to Chernarus, giving you more ways to grow food, recover seeds, and build a reliable garden.

Whether you’re planting your first radish, saving seeds for the next season, or setting up a full production garden, this guide covers what you need to know.

Getting Started

Find Seeds and Supplies

Seed packs can be found as farm loot or purchased from the Consumables Trader at Scorched Isle Market.

Start small, pick a sensible location, and give your first plot room to grow. A little preparation saves a surprising amount of running around later.

Survivor Tip: Carry a few extra seeds and basic supplies. Gardens are much less forgiving when you arrive with exactly one of everything.

What You’ll Need

  • Seeds or seed packs.
  • Gardening tools for preparing and maintaining a plot.
  • Water for keeping the crop healthy.
  • A knife or suitable blade for harvest and seed recovery.
  • Enough inventory space to carry the harvest.

Planting Your First Crop

Step 01

Prepare Your Garden

Choose a suitable location and use normal DayZ gardening tools to prepare a plot. Convenient access makes watering and harvesting much easier.

Step 02

Get Your Seeds Ready

Open the seed pack to receive the planting material. Depending on the crop, that may be seeds, cloves, rhizomes, or seed pieces.

Step 03

Plant Your Crop

Place the correct planting material into an open plot slot and confirm the planting action.

Step 04

Water and Maintain

Water the crop and check its condition while it grows. Growth takes real time, so keep the plot supplied and healthy.

Step 05

Harvest Your Produce

Harvest when the plant reaches its harvest stage. Leaving a mature crop unattended can cost you the work you put into it.

What You Get: Fresh produce for eating, cooking, selling, and selected seed-recovery actions. Plant multiple crops when you have room so one harvest is not carrying your entire food plan.

Harvesting & Preparing Your Produce

Watermelon and Cantaloupe

Use a blade to process these harvests into slices and recover their seeds where the applicable action is available.

Sweet Potatoes

Peel sweet potatoes when you want them ready for food preparation. Keep some unpeeled if you plan to cut them into planting pieces.

Cooking Your Harvest

Cooking options vary by item. Check the actions on the produce before committing the whole harvest to a recipe.

Do not cook everything first: Some crops need to stay raw for seed recovery.

Saving Seeds for Your Next Garden

How Seed Saving Works

  1. Harvest the crop and set aside the raw produce.
  2. Use a knife or suitable blade and look for the relevant action.
  3. Recover the seeds or planting material.
  4. Store the result for your next plot or replant it when ready.
Survivor Tip: Keep raw harvest separate from food you plan to cook. A full stomach is useful, but so is having something left to plant.

Supported Recovery Methods

CropRecovery Action
BarleyThresh to recover planting material.
SorghumThresh to recover planting material.
PeasShell to recover planting material.
PeanutsShell to recover planting material.
GarlicSplit into cloves.
Sweet PotatoesCut into seed pieces.
SunflowersRecover seeds from the heads.
GinsengUse the berry-to-seed recipe.

Other Crops and Important Rules

Other supported crops may have their own recovery actions, so inspect the raw harvest before cooking or selling it.

Crops Without Harvest-to-Seed Recipes

The following crops do not have harvest-to-seed recipes in BTA's installed version. Their planting material must be obtained from seed packs or the Consumables Trader.

  • Beets
  • Cabbage
  • Carrots
  • Collards
  • Hops
  • Leeks
  • Onions
  • Radishes
  • Turnips
  • Seed recovery consumes the harvest used in the action.
  • Cooked produce cannot be used for seed recovery.
  • Different crops use different actions, and not every crop supports recovery.
  • Keep raw harvest separate from anything intended for the stove.

Finding Wild Ginseng

Where to Look

Wild ginseng can appear in broadleaf woodland and shares the existing mushroom-spawn event. It may be found fresh, dried, or rotten.

Garden-grown ginseng starts fresh. Finding one plant does not guarantee that another will appear immediately afterward.

Wild Ginseng Conditions

ConditionWhat It Means
FreshFreshly generated wild ginseng.
DriedWild ginseng generated in a dried condition.
RottenSpoiled ginseng; check its condition before use.
Survivor Tip: Check the woodland as you travel, but do not plan an entire food supply around finding one particular plant in one particular patch.

Buying & Selling at the Consumables Trader

The Consumables Trader is at Scorched Isle Market. The gardening categories currently available are:

  • Geb's Plants - Seed Packs
  • Geb's Plants - Seeds
  • Geb's Plants - Produce
  • Geb's Plants - Garden Guide

These categories support buying and selling with unlimited configured stock.

Geb’s Garden Guide

Garden Guides can be found on shelves in farm, village, and town buildings, or purchased from the Consumables Trader.

Hold the guide, unfold it, and read it for the broader planting information it contains. The original mod guide covers a wider crop selection; BTA Chernarus currently supports the 26 crops listed above.

BTA-specific information: Cannabis, Coca, Poppy, Tobacco, and their related Geb chains are not part of the BTA plant system described here. The separate BTA drug-production system has its own Drug Running guide.

Gardening & Terje Farming Skills

Gardening actions can contribute to the Terje Farming skill. Preparing plots, planting, watering, fertilizing, and harvesting are all part of working a garden.

As the skill develops, it can improve yields, freshness, growth speed, and efficiency. Skill levels do not automatically purchase perks, so check the Terje system and choose the benefits that suit your survivor.

Important Gardening Tips & Reminders

Keep the Garden Healthy

  • Do not assume a mature plant will remain harvestable indefinitely.
  • Save eligible seed material before cooking the full harvest.
  • Store harvest and supplies somewhere sensible. Ground items are subject to cleanup.
  • The presence of an ingredient does not guarantee that every brewing or crafting system uses it.

Cleanup Timers

These are configured cleanup times, not a promise that an item will remain fresh, harvestable, or stored for its entire lifetime.

Quick Start Checklist

  1. Find or buy seeds.
  2. Gather gardening supplies and prepare a plot.
  3. Open the pack and plant the right material.
  4. Water the crop and give it time to grow.
  5. Harvest at the right stage.
  6. Save eligible planting material before cooking.
  7. Prepare, cook, sell, or store the harvest.
  8. Replant and expand when your first garden is working.

One Seed at a Time

The world may be covered in ashes, but a good garden still gives people something to build around.

Grow food, save seeds, share the surplus, and keep one more season in mind than the people who came before you. That is how a patch of soil becomes a home.

Get planting. The next harvest is closer than it looks.
