01 // First Steps

Welcome to the Underground

Every underground empire starts with the right connections—or, in this case, a key pulled off an infected.

Drug Supply Cache Keys open two kinds of supply containers across Chernarus: hidden Drug Supply Caches inside industrial buildings and Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrops. Both can hold valuable chemicals, equipment and production materials.

Finding Your Supplies

Step 01

Find a key

Search the infected for Drug Supply Cache Keys and hang onto them. One key works for both types of supply container.

Step 02

Put that key to work

Use it on a Drug Supply Cache inside an industrial building or on an Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrop when you find one. No separate keys are needed.

Step 03

Build your empire

Gather your chemicals, equipment, and production materials, then put them to work. Every successful operation has to start somewhere!

ONE KEY, TWO OPPORTUNITIES

One Drug Supply Cache Key opens either an industrial Drug Supply Cache or an Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrop. Keep one handy—you never know when your next payday might fall from the sky.

Packaging supply: Empty ziplock baggies are available from the Drug Ingredients trader with infinite stock. Boris sells supplies to players and does not buy them back.

As you progress, you will work with equipment such as vanilla knives, a Production Table, a Lab Station, a Chemical Barrel and an Industrial Pill Press. The recipes below tell you what goes where and what comes out.

02 // The Green Business

Cannabis

Grow it, dry it, trim it and keep every strain where it belongs. The plant is only the beginning; the real work starts when the harvest hits your table.

Grow and Harvest

  1. Find the strain you want in a seed pack and plant the seeds in a fertilized garden plot, greenhouse, Grow Tent or Hydroponic System.
  2. Water the slot and wait for the plant to reach its harvest stage.
  3. Use the Harvest Crop prompt to collect Bud Branches.
What you get: Fresh Bud Branches. Branch yields vary by strain and server setup.

Manual Trimming

Use a Bud Branch with most vanilla knives to trim it by hand. This is the simple route when you have branches, a knife and no patience for machinery.

What you get: Buds for the next stage. Output varies by strain.

Drying Rack

  • Load up to 24 cannabis Bud Branches.
  • The rack also has 8 slots for Coca Leaves.
  • Drying can improve production yields.
After drying: Collect the dried output beneath the rack once the process finishes.

Bud Trimmer

  • Load dried Bud Branches into up to 24 slots.
  • On Chernarus, attach a car battery before starting the trimmer.
  • Different cannabis strains can be processed at the same time.
After trimming: Collect the Buds from the clear bin beneath the machine.

Craft Small Nugs

Break down one Bud with most vanilla knives to produce one stack of 14 Small Nugs. Use them for blunts or for packing the Bong.

What you get: Small Nugs.

Craft Blunts

  1. Unpack a Backwoodz 3Pack to get three Backwoodz Cigars.
  2. Combine one Backwoodz Cigar with three Small Nugs from the same strain.
What you get: One blunt made from the matching strain.

Craft Zips

  • 1 Bud + 1 Bud = 1 Zip
  • 1 Zip + 1 standard knife = 2 Buds
THC TIP: Individual buds retain their THC information, but Zips and Bricks don't display a combined THC percentage. Keep your strains organized when packaging.

Craft Cannabis Bricks

  1. Use a Production Table and open the Cannabis Products attachment slots.
  2. Load 16 Buds of one strain: eight slots containing two buds each.
  3. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie in your hands and approach the table.
  4. Select the craft prompt when it appears.
What you get: One packaged Cannabis Brick.
Strain check: Keep the same strain together for every recipe. Drying times, plant yields and processing output can be customized, so treat the machine prompt and the current Chernarus setup as the final word.

03 // The Makeshift Laboratory

Chemical Production

Three meth batches, several ways to break them down and enough attachment slots to make a tidy survivor question every life choice. Keep each variant separate from tray to brick.

Meth tray breakdown: You can break a tray down by hand with common knives or hand tools, but the amount of shards can vary. On Chernarus, attach the tray to the Production Table and approach it with a hammer for the table recipe. The active setup returns five stacks of 20 shards—100 shards total—for that tray variant.

Heisenberg Blue

  • Ammonia
  • Sodium Hydroxide
  • Pseudoephedrine
  • Acetone
  • Hydrofluoric Acid
  • Gas Stove attached to the Lab Station

Attach the ingredients, then approach the Lab Station with a vanilla lighter or Blowtorch.

What you get: One Heisenberg Blue meth tray.

Peanut Butter (Biker Batch)

  • Ammonia
  • Pseudoephedrine
  • Bleach 01
  • Brake Fluid
  • Battery Acid 02
  • Ammonium Nitrate
  • Propane tank, any size
  • Gas Stove attached to the Lab Station

Attach the ingredients, then use a vanilla lighter or Blowtorch at the station.

What you get: One Peanut Butter meth tray.

Pink Nesquik

  • Ammonia
  • Pseudoephedrine
  • Bleach 02
  • Battery Acid 01
  • Isopropyl Alcohol
  • Gas Stove attached to the Lab Station

Attach the ingredients, then approach with a vanilla lighter or Blowtorch.

What you get: One Pink Nesquik meth tray.

Craft Meth Bundles

Combine 1 Shard + 1 Shard from the same meth variant.

What you get: One Meth Bundle.

Craft Meth Pounds

Combine 5 Shards + 5 Shards from the same meth variant.

What you get: One Pound.

Craft Meth Bricks

  1. Break down the matching tray into 100 shards: five stacks of 20.
  2. Load the five stacks into the five Meth Products attachment slots on the Production Table.
  3. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie and approach the table.
What you get: One Meth Brick of that matching variant.
Do not mix variants: Blue, Peanut Butter and Pink materials must stay with their own kind all the way through bundling, pounds and bricks. The table is not a chemistry teacher; it will not correct your homework.

04 // White Gold

Cocaine

Start with Coca Seeds, turn mature plants into leaves and choose between the quick hand-crafting route or the higher-output Chemical Barrel route.

Grow and Harvest Coca

  1. Plant Coca Seeds in a prepared growing slot.
  2. Water the plant and wait for the harvest stage.
  3. Harvest the mature Coca Bush to collect Coca Leaves.
What you get: Fresh Coca Leaves. A Drying Rack can turn them into dried leaves for the higher-output barrel route.

Hand-Craft Coke Piles

  1. Combine Coca Leaves with Acetone to make Coke Piles.
  2. Combine 1 Coke Pile + 1 Coke Pile to make one Cocaine Bundle.
What you get: Cocaine Bundles ready for packaging.

Chemical Barrel Requirements

  • One Chemical Barrel
  • Sulfuric Acid
  • Eight stacks of 15 Coca Leaves
  • All leaves must be fresh or all dried—do not mix them
  • A Jerry Can containing gasoline
Output: Dried leaves produce a higher output by default, while fresh leaves remain the simpler route.

Run the Chemical Barrel

  1. Attach the leaves and Sulfuric Acid to their matching Chemical Barrel slots.
  2. Hold the gasoline Jerry Can in your hands.
  3. Approach the barrel and select the manufacturing prompt.
  4. Collect the finished product from the top of the barrel when the process ends.
Fuel note: The active Chernarus recipe checks for at least 1,000 gasoline units in the Jerry Can for the barrel run.

Craft Cocaine Bricks

  1. Use five Cocaine Bundles of the same type.
  2. Load them into the five Cocaine Products attachment slots on the Production Table.
  3. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie and approach the table.
What you get: One packaged Cocaine Brick.
Fresh or dried, not both: The Chemical Barrel will not accept a mixed stack of fresh and dried Coca Leaves. Pick one route before you fill all eight slots.

05 // The Underground Pharmacy

Ecstasy

Color, ingredient order and the right equipment all matter. Treat these recipes like a checklist, not a suggestion from a man who has been awake for three days.

Hand Crafting

  1. 1 Color Powder Pouch + 1 Plain Bag of Binding Agent = Colored Binding Agent.
  2. 1 MDMA Pack + 1 matching Colored Binding Agent = 200 pills under the documented default recipe.
Reminder: The exact output can be changed for a server, so use the current Chernarus setup and crafting prompt.

Hydraulic Pill Press

Use the same MDMA Pack and matching Colored Binding Agent, then approach the press with a wrench.

Chernarus currently: The active press recipes produce 20 stacks of 50 pills—1,000 pills total. Staff can change this output.

Industrial Pill Press

Use the same color ingredients and a wrench. The Industrial Pill Press automatically produces Thizz Bundles instead of leaving you to bundle every pill by hand.

Chernarus currently: One press craft produces five Thizz Bundles. This is not a Production Table packaging craft, so it does not need an extra Empty Ziplock Baggie.

Purple Thizz Slots

  1. Slot 1: Bitcoin — full stack of 50
  2. Slot 2: Morty — full stack of 50
  3. Slot 3: Rolex — full stack of 50
  4. Slot 4: Orange Biohazard — full stack of 50
Order matters: Use this exact order.

Green Thizz Slots

  1. Slot 1: Butterfly — full stack of 50
  2. Slot 2: Rick — full stack of 50
  3. Slot 3: Playboy — full stack of 50
  4. Slot 4: White Spy — full stack of 50
Order matters: Use this exact order.

Blue Thizz Slots

  1. Slot 1: Mitsubishi — full stack of 50
  2. Slot 2: Naked Lady — full stack of 50
  3. Slot 3: Mac Dre — full stack of 50
  4. Slot 4: Pink Discord — full stack of 50
Order matters: Use this exact order.

Yellow Thizz Slots

  1. Slot 1: Round Star — full stack of 50
  2. Slot 2: Jake the Dog — full stack of 50
  3. Slot 3: Big Bird — full stack of 50
  4. Slot 4: Red Che — full stack of 50
Order matters: Use this exact order.

Bricks and Transport

  1. Load five Thizz Bundles of the same color into the Production Table and hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie to craft an Ecstasy Brick.
  2. Use two MDMA Packs at the Production Table with one Empty Ziplock Baggie to craft an MDMA Brick.
  3. Place five Thizz Bundles into a Thizz Boat Pouch to carry the equivalent of 1,000 pills.
Do not confuse them: A Thizz Boat Pouch transports bundles; it is not an Ecstasy Brick.
Exact order, full stacks: Colored Thizz bundles need four full 50-pill stacks in the matching order. If one slot is wrong, the underground pharmacy has decided you are not ready.

06 // The Heavy Stuff

Opium, Heroin & Fentanyl

These paths are active on Chernarus and run through the Chemical Barrel before they reach the Production Table. Bring the right chemicals, keep the process separate and do not improvise with mystery powder.

Grow Poppies

  1. Plant Poppy Seeds in a prepared growing slot.
  2. Water the plant and wait for the harvest stage.
  3. Harvest the plant to collect Opium Knots.
What you get: Opium Knots for Brown Heroin or Black Tar production.

Brown Heroin

  • 3 stacks of 10 Opium Knots
  • Acetic Anhydride
  • Baking Soda
  • A Jerry Can containing gasoline

Attach the ingredients to the Chemical Barrel, then approach it with the Jerry Can in hand.

What you get: Brown Heroin Piles.

Black Tar

  • 3 stacks of 10 Opium Knots
  • Acetic Anhydride
  • A Jerry Can containing gasoline

Attach the ingredients and approach the barrel with the Jerry Can in hand.

Chernarus note: The active Black Tar recipe uses Acetic Anhydride only; it does not require Baking Soda.

Fentanyl

  • Precursor 209
  • Precursor 510
  • Precursor 707
  • Precursor 916
  • A Jerry Can containing gasoline

Load one of each precursor into the four precursor slots, then approach the Chemical Barrel with the Jerry Can in hand.

What you get: Fentanyl Powder Piles.

Package Brown Heroin

Load two stacks of 10 Brown Heroin Piles into the two Production Table powder slots. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie and approach the table.

What you get: One Powdered Heroin Brick.

Package Fentanyl

Load two stacks of 10 Fentanyl Powder Piles into the two Fent Products slots. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie and approach the table.

What you get: One Fentanyl Brick.

Package Black Tar

Load five stacks of 10 Black Tar into the five Black Tar/Knot slots. Hold one Empty Ziplock Baggie and approach the table.

What you get: One Black Tar Brick.
Handle with care: Keep the three production paths separate. Brown Heroin uses both Acetic Anhydride and Baking Soda; Black Tar uses Acetic Anhydride only on Chernarus; Fentanyl uses the four listed precursors.

07 // The Blotter Lab

LSD Lab

The LSD Lab Station is active on Chernarus. It is a smaller, time-based process: attach the required ingredients, turn on the heaters and let the station do the suspiciously quiet work.

What You'll Need

  • LSD Lab Station
  • Pyrex Beaker
  • Precursor 510 + Precursor 707
  • Blank Blotter Sheets
  • 9V Battery as the power source

Start the Process

  1. Attach each item to its matching station slot.
  2. Use the Turn On action to start the heating elements.
  3. Wait for the time-based process to finish.
  4. Collect the output from the station attachment slot.
What you get: LSD Blotter Sheets. The current Chernarus setup returns one sheet per run; timing and output can be changed later.

08 // Time to Get Paid

Cashing Out

Raw materials are not the payday. Finished, properly packaged product is what gets you to the counter.

Meet Hassan at Cinder Market

Hassan is the Drug Production Trader at Cinder Market • Eastern Sea. His market runs on USD: he buys finished and packaged drug products, cannabis nuggets and other accepted production goods, and sells supplies for the next run.

Need to restock? Hassan’s shelves include zip-lock baggies, storage pallets, drying racks, hydroponic systems, lab and production stations, Industrial pill presses and bud trimmers. Check his live trader profile before making the trip and bring the product you actually intend to sell.

The survivor’s rule: build the product first, pack it correctly, then make the crossing. Arriving with half a recipe and a hopeful expression is not a recognized currency.

09 // Hard-Earned Advice

Tips From the Underground

A few small checks can save a full run, a rare ingredient and the awkward walk home.

Before You Craft

  • Don't mix your strains! Keep cannabis buds from the same strain together. Nobody wants a mystery brick.
  • Keep your products organized. When a recipe calls for matching ingredients, make sure your chemical shards, bundles, or cocaine bundles are all the same type.
  • Colors matter! When crafting colored Thizz, pay attention to the exact order of your ingredients. Tossing everything together and hoping for the best won't cut it.
  • Bring the right tools. Nothing ruins a productive afternoon like getting halfway through a recipe and realizing you left your equipment at home.

Pack It Up, Move It Out

  • Stock up on baggies! Packaging products at the Production Table requires one Empty Ziplock Baggie per craft.
  • Duct tape won't save you. You can't substitute duct tape for an Empty Ziplock Baggie when packaging products at the Production Table.
  • Small batches work differently. Hand-crafted bags, bundles, and pounds follow their own recipes. Check the individual crafting instructions for what you'll need.
  • Using the Industrial Pill Press? Follow its specific crafting requirements. You won't need the extra Empty Ziplock Baggie used for Production Table packaging.

A Word to the Wise

Not every recipe you find out in the wasteland is going to work the same way here. When packaging products at the Production Table, you'll need one Empty Ziplock Baggie per craft. Duct tape isn't going to cut it.

Breaking down meth trays at the Production Table? Bring a hammer.

The official iM7s Wiki is a great place to learn more, but when something works differently on Beneath the Ashes, follow the Chernarus instructions in this handbook.
