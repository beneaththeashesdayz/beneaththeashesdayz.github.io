01 // First Steps

Welcome to the Underground

Every underground empire starts with the right connections—or, in this case, a key pulled off an infected.

Drug Supply Cache Keys unlock hidden supply caches tucked away inside industrial buildings AND Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrops that come down across Chernarus.

Both are opportunities to get your hands on valuable chemicals, equipment, and production materials. Keep your eyes open and your pockets ready!

Finding Your Supplies

Step 01

Find a key

Search the infected for Drug Supply Cache Keys. Hang onto them! These little beauties open more than just hidden supply caches.

Step 02

Put that key to work

Found a Drug Supply Cache inside an industrial building? Unlock it! Spot an Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrop? That same key will get you inside. Two ways to score supplies, one handy little key.

Step 03

Build your empire

Gather your chemicals, equipment, and production materials, then put them to work. Every successful operation has to start somewhere!

ONE KEY, TWO OPPORTUNITIES

Your Drug Supply Cache Key opens both industrial Drug Supply Caches and Ash 'n' Grass Supply Airdrops. Keep one handy—you never know when your next payday might fall from the sky.

Packaging supply: Empty ziplock baggies are available from the Drug Ingredients trader with infinite stock. Boris sells supplies to players and does not buy them back.

As you progress, you will work with equipment such as vanilla knives, a Production Table, a Lab Station, a Chemical Barrel and an Industrial Pill Press. The recipes below tell you what goes where and what comes out.

02 // The Green Business

Cannabis

Keep each strain together and work through the plant from branch to bud, then decide whether you are making something to smoke, split or package.

Trim Bud Branches

Use a bud branch with most vanilla knives to produce buds. Drying racks can increase output, and yield varies by strain.

What you get: Buds for the next stage.

Craft Small Nugs

Break down a bud with most vanilla knives to produce a stack of 14 small nugs for blunts or bong packing.

What you get: Small nugs.

Craft Blunts

  1. Unpack a Backwoodz 3Pack to find three cigars.
  2. Combine one Backwoodz cigar with three small nugs of the same strain.
What you get: A blunt made from the matching strain.

Craft Zips

Combine buds into a zip. When you need the buds back, use a vanilla knife to split the zip into two buds.

What you get: A zip, or two buds when split.

Craft Bricks

Place 16 buds of the same strain into the Production Table with one empty ziplock baggie for the packaging craft.

What you get: A packaged cannabis brick.
Strain check: Keep the same strain together when crafting. If the materials do not match, the recipe is not going to do you any favors.

03 // The Makeshift Laboratory

Chemical Production

Ready to get your hands dirty? Gather the right equipment, work through the product stages and keep your ingredients organized. Chemical work rewards patience more than panic.

Lab Station Cooking

Attach the required ingredients to the Lab Station, then use the tool item to craft the selected meth tray.

What you get: The selected meth tray.

Break Down Trays

Break trays down with tools or at the Production Table to recover 100 shards, equivalent to a brick quantity.

What you get: Shards ready for further production.

Craft Bundles

Combine one shard with one shard to create a meth bundle.

What you get: A meth bundle.

Craft Pounds

Combine two stacks of five shards to create one pound.

What you get: One pound.

Craft Bricks

Use 100 shards of the same type at the Production Table with one empty ziplock baggie for the packaging craft.

What you get: A packaged chemical brick.
Other Production Table packaging: Powdered heroin, fentanyl and black tar brick recipes follow the same Chernarus rule: one empty ziplock baggie per packaging craft.

04 // White Gold

Cocaine

There are two routes from coca leaves to finished product: the straightforward hand-crafting path and the Chemical Barrel method.

Hand Crafting

  1. Combine coca leaves with acetone to create coke piles.
  2. Combine coke piles into cocaine bundles.
What you get: Cocaine bundles ready for packaging.

Chemical Barrel

Use a Chemical Barrel, sulfuric acid and a full Jerry Can. Attach the required coca leaves, then approach the barrel with the Jerry Can in hand.

What you get: The barrel method’s higher-output cocaine production path.

Craft Cocaine Bricks

Use five cocaine bundles of the same type at the Production Table with one empty ziplock baggie for the packaging craft.

What you get: A packaged cocaine brick.
Keep product types together: the brick recipe calls for five bundles of the same type. Mixing types is a great way to waste a trip and impress nobody.

05 // The Underground Pharmacy

Ecstasy

Color, ingredient order and the right equipment all matter here. Treat the recipe like a checklist, not a suggestion from a man who has been awake for three days.

Hand Crafting

Combine a color pouch with a binding agent, then combine the required MDMA packs and pills to create the matching variant.

What you get: The matching colored product variant.

Industrial Pill Press

Deploy the Industrial Pill Press and use the matching ingredients with a wrench in hand. It can automatically produce Thizz Bundles.

Important: The Industrial Pill Press does not require the Production Table ziplock packaging change.

Slot Order Matters

Purple, green, blue and yellow Thizz recipes each require their own exact item order.

Before crafting: Check the color and slot order.

Bricks & Transport

  1. Five same-color Thizz Bundles produce an Ecstasy Brick.
  2. Two MDMA Packs produce an MDMA Brick.
  3. Five Thizz Bundles can also be packed for transport.
Packaging rule: Bring one empty ziplock baggie per Production Table packaging craft.

06 // Time to Get Paid

Cashing Out

Raw materials are not the payday. Finished, properly packaged product is what gets you to the counter.

Meet Hassan at Cinder Market

Hassan is the Drug Production Trader at Cinder Market • Eastern Sea. His market runs on USD: he buys finished and packaged drug products, cannabis nuggets and other accepted production goods, and sells supplies for the next run.

Need to restock? Hassan’s shelves include zip-lock baggies, storage pallets, drying racks, hydroponic systems, lab and production stations, Industrial pill presses and bud trimmers. Check his live trader profile before making the trip and bring the product you actually intend to sell.

The survivor’s rule: build the product first, pack it correctly, then make the crossing. Arriving with half a recipe and a hopeful expression is not a recognized currency.

07 // Hard-Earned Advice

Tips From the Underground

A few small checks can save a full run, a rare ingredient and the awkward walk home.

Before You Craft

  • Don't mix your strains! Keep cannabis buds from the same strain together. Nobody wants a mystery brick.
  • Keep your products organized. When a recipe calls for matching ingredients, make sure your chemical shards, bundles, or cocaine bundles are all the same type.
  • Colors matter! When crafting colored Thizz, pay attention to the exact order of your ingredients. Tossing everything together and hoping for the best won't cut it.
  • Bring the right tools. Nothing ruins a productive afternoon like getting halfway through a recipe and realizing you left your equipment at home.

Pack It Up, Move It Out

  • Stock up on baggies! Packaging products at the Production Table requires one Empty Ziplock Baggie per craft.
  • Duct tape won't save you. You can't substitute duct tape for an Empty Ziplock Baggie when packaging products at the Production Table.
  • Small batches work differently. Hand-crafted bags, bundles, and pounds follow their own recipes. Check the individual crafting instructions for what you'll need.
  • Using the Industrial Pill Press? Follow its specific crafting requirements. You won't need the extra Empty Ziplock Baggie used for Production Table packaging.
