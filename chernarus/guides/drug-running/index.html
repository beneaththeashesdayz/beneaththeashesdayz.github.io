Gather hidden supplies, process materials and build an in-game production operation across Chernarus. Recipes and requirements may change, so check the official wiki for the latest details.

Gathering Your Supplies

Keys to drug supply caches can be found on infected. Use them to unlock supply caches in industrial buildings and recover chemicals, equipment and production materials.

Supply aircraft may also bring a Drug Supply Airdrop to Chernarus. Drops rotate, so there is no guarantee that every shipment will contain the materials you need.

Start with the basics: find a cache key or respond to an airdrop, then build your operation around the materials you recover.

Cannabis Recipes

Keep the same strain of buds together when crafting. Cannabis quantities include bud branches, buds, small nugs, zips and bricks.

Trim Bud Branches
Use a bud branch with most vanilla knives to produce buds. Drying racks can increase output, and yield varies by strain.
Craft Small Nugs
Break down a bud with most vanilla knives to produce a stack of 14 small nugs for blunts or bong packing.
Craft Blunts
Unpack a Backwoodz 3Pack to find three cigars, then combine one Backwoodz cigar with three small nugs of the same strain.
Craft Zips
Combine buds into a zip, then use a vanilla knife to split the zip back into two buds when needed.
Craft Bricks
Place 16 buds of the same strain into the Production Table, using duct tape or bags as required.

Chemical Recipes

Complex chemical products are produced with Tree's IAT_CraftingPlus. Chemical quantities include shards, bundles, pounds and bricks.

Lab Station Cooking
Attach the required ingredients to the Lab Station and use the tool item to craft the selected meth tray.
Break Down Trays
Break trays down with tools or at the Production Table to recover 100 shards, equivalent to a brick quantity.
Craft Bundles
Combine one shard with one shard to create a meth bundle.
Craft Pounds
Combine two stacks of five shards to create one pound.
Craft Bricks
Use 100 shards of the same type at the Production Table with duct tape or a Ziplock bag.

Cocaine Recipes

Cocaine has a simple hand-crafting path and a higher-output Chemical Barrel method. Dried coca leaves produce better output through the barrel method.

Hand Crafting
Combine coca leaves with acetone to create coke piles, then combine coke piles into cocaine bundles.
Chemical Barrel
Use a Chemical Barrel, sulfuric acid and a full Jerry Can. Attach the required coca leaves and approach the barrel with the Jerry Can in hand.
Craft Cocaine Bricks
Use five cocaine bundles of the same type at the Production Table with duct tape or bags.

Ecstasy Recipes

Ecstasy production uses colored binding agents, MDMA packs and pills. Choose between hand crafting and the Industrial Pill Press.

Hand Crafting
Combine a color pouch with a binding agent, then combine the required MDMA packs and pills to create the matching variant.
Pill Press
Deploy the Industrial Pill Press and use the matching ingredients with a wrench in hand. It can automatically produce Thizz Bundles.
Slot Order Matters
Purple, green, blue and yellow Thizz recipes each require their own exact item order.
Bricks & Transport
Five same-color Thizz Bundles produce an Ecstasy Brick. Two MDMA Packs produce an MDMA Brick. Five Thizz Bundles can also be packed for transport.

Recipe Reminder

Strain, type and slot order matter. Read the recipe requirements carefully before combining materials, and use the official wiki whenever the server’s crafting version changes.
