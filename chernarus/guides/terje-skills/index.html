Terje
Skills

Terje Skills is back on BTA Chernarus! Your adventures can now build more than an impressive collection of questionable loot. As you carry out survival activities, you’ll develop skills and earn perks that help shape your survivor.

How to Open Your Skills

  1. Press Tab to open your inventory.
  2. Scroll to the very bottom of your inventory to find your skills.
  3. Explore your progress and available perks.
  4. Read each perk’s description, requirements and point cost before upgrading.
Find somewhere safe before browsing. The infected will not politely wait while you plan your character.

Skills, Levels and Perks

  • Related activities earn experience in a skill.
  • Levels measure progress and unlock access to perks.
  • Perks provide specific benefits and may have level requirements, point costs and upgrade stages.
  • Some improvements come from skill levels; others require spending perk points.
Important: Leveling a skill does not automatically purchase its perks.

Seven Skills Enabled on BTA

Each skill improves through related survival activities and supports a different part of life in Chernarus.

Metabolism

Train by: Eating and drinking.

Supports: Efficient food and hydration use, hunger and thirst.

Athletics

Train by: Running over distance.

Supports: Stamina, endurance, movement and stamina management.

Strength

Train through: Melee combat and physical tool use, such as chopping wood or extracting stone.

Supports: Carrying capacity and melee effectiveness.

Stealth

Train through: Crouched travel and successful stealth kills.

Supports: Quieter movement and avoiding attention.

Survival

Train by: Staying alive, killing infected, starting fires and cooking food on a stick.

Supports: Environmental survival, cold resistance, fire use, equipment durability and bushcraft.

Hunting

Train through: Hunting, butchering animals and successful trapping.

Supports: Meat processing, knife use, skinning and trapping.

Farming

Train by: Preparing garden plots, planting, watering, fertilizing and harvesting.

Supports: Harvest yields, crop freshness, growth speed and gardening efficiency.

Crafting That Requires Perks

BTA requires the first purchased rank of the relevant perk for the recipes below. Both perks are under Survival.

Ancestral Technologies

  • Hand-drill kits.
  • Higher ranks improve the number of uses of crafted kits.

Bushcrafter

  • Stone knives and bone knives.
  • Stone-tipped and bone-tipped spears.
  • Improvised arrows, including bone-tipped arrows.
  • Nailed and barbed-wire baseball bats.
  • Crafted ghillie rifle wraps, bushrags, hoods, tops and full suits.
  • Improvised leather courier bags, sacks and backpacks.
  • Higher ranks improve crafted item quality.
Check the perk first: Raising Survival alone does not purchase the required perk. If the correct ingredients do not produce the recipe, check the perk requirements. This does not mean every crafting recipe on the server requires a perk. These mappings come from the mod’s published recipe code; do not extend them to unrelated recipes or other mods.

Titles generally follow:

  • “[Subject] Fundamentals”

Read the item description: It shows the book’s skill, reward and level range. Two middle-tier Hunting book labels are swapped in the published mod, so the description’s level range is more reliable than the title or cover number.
How to read
  1. Hold the book in your hands.
  2. Use the Read action and let it finish.
  3. Check the relevant skill afterward.

Reading takes approximately 15 seconds.

Confirmed BTA Reading Rules

  • Each specific book type or title awards experience only once per player profile; a duplicate copy does not provide another boost.
  • Different tiers are different books and can help when the player meets their level requirements.
  • Successful reading consumes the book.
  • Already-known, too-difficult or too-basic books award no experience and are not consumed by that unsuccessful attempt.
  • Consider giving unneeded duplicates to another survivor.

Death and Progress

  • Death does not automatically reset all skills and perks to zero.
  • Some skill experience is deducted on death.
  • Do not assume every level or perk remains unaffected.

Manual Perk Resets

Reset cost: Manual resets are enabled, but resetting returns only 50% of the affected skill’s XP.

This is separate from the death penalty. Make the cost clear before choosing to reset a skill.

Troubleshooting and Support

If a skill, perk, book or recipe is not behaving as expected, open a ticket and include:

  • The skill, perk, book or recipe involved.
  • What you were doing and approximately when.
  • Expected versus actual behavior.
  • Whether the issue repeats.
  • Screenshots or video where possible.
