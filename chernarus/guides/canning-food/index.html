Food Preservation

Canning
Food

Fresh food does not stay fresh for long in Chernarus. Learn how to turn cooked food and empty tin cans into shelf-stable supplies for the road, your base and the winter ahead.

How It Works

01Boil Food
Cook meat, fruit, vegetables or mushrooms first.
02Prepare the Can
Use an empty tin can of the appropriate size. No water is needed.
03Add Food
Place the cooked food into the empty can.
04Seal the Can
Close your inventory, hold the can and use the Preserve action.

Can Sizes

  • Large Can · 400 ml: four slots; holds one or two pieces of meat.
  • Small Can · 200 ml: two slots; holds one piece of meat, fruit, vegetable or mushroom.
  • Large cans do not accept fruit, vegetables or mushrooms.

Extra Uses

  • Empty tin cans can also be used to carry water.
  • Empty tin cans can be used to carry gasoline.
  • Keep different sizes available so you are ready for whatever food you bring home.

Finding Empty Cans

Scavenge abandoned homes, camps, factories and forgotten settlements across Chernarus. Most survivors leave empty cans behind, making them easy to overlook and valuable to anyone planning ahead.

Need supplies now? The Consumables Trader at Scorched Isle Market may have empty cans available. Stock is limited, so scavenging remains the most reliable way to build a reserve.

Survivor Reminder

A full stomach gets you through today. A shelf full of canned food gets you through the winter.
