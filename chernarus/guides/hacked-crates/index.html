Dynamic Events

Hacked
Crates

High-risk, high-reward events that draw survivors toward rare gear, valuable collectables, exclusive weapons and Research Access Keycards.

How It Works

01 · Find it

Locate a Hacked Crate somewhere in Chernarus. Events can appear across the map, so stay alert while travelling.

02 · Hack it

Interact with the crate to begin the hacking sequence. The crate remains vulnerable while the hack is in progress.

03 · Defend it

Protect the crate while it unlocks. Other survivors may be watching, approaching or competing for the same reward.

04 · Claim it

Once the hack is complete, take your loot before someone else does. The opportunity is not secure until you leave.

Survivor Tips

  • Bring ammunition and prepare for a fight.
  • Expect competition—these events are visible opportunities.
  • Keep your inventory ready for valuable rewards.
  • Research Access Keycards open secured Ember Research Facilities.
Every crate is another chance, but you are never alone out there.

Exclusive Hacked Crate Loot

MMG GearExclusive military gear and attachments to build out your loadout.
Vyse CollectablesRare collectables that can be sold to Kaito at Cinder Market for premium prices.
Exclusive WeaponsWeapons not normally found in standard military loot, including LMGs, rifles, shotguns and pistols.

Research Access Keycards

Hacked Crates can contain Research Access Keycards used to unlock secured Ember Research Facilities across Chernarus. Keycards are not tiered—each color opens a different facility, and every color is worth using.
