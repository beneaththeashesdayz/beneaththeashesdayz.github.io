Team Rocket Supply Drops

Supply drops are live.

Every 30–45 minutes, Team Rocket will attempt to smuggle a shipment into Chernarus.

Supply drops can contain Poké Balls from every tier, including rare Elite and Master tier balls.

Infected may carry Team Rocket Cargo Keys. Find a key, watch the skies, track down the drop, and unlock the shipment.
