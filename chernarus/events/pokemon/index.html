The Siege of Rocket Island

“Prepare for trouble!”
“And make it double!”

The isolated concrete monolith of Prison Island usually echoed with the groans of the dead and the cold whistling of the southern sea winds. Today, it vibrates to a different rhythm: makeshift machinery, clanking sheet metal, and Team Rocket's arrival motif.

The Heist of the Century

Team Rocket never planned to end up in the post-apocalyptic wasteland of Chernarus. A botched dimensional telemetry device invented by Professor Nanabu pulled them through a rift during an attempted heist in the Sinnoh region.

After realizing the peninsula was largely devoid of local law enforcement—and crawling with rare, bizarre creatures they classified as “mutated regional variants”—they saw an opportunity. If survivors were too busy fighting over canned beans and military tents to notice, Team Rocket would rule the wasteland.

Their master plan has two pillars:

  • The Loot Cache: Seize the multi-level penitentiary, barricade the connecting land bridge and causeway with concertina wire, landmines, and watchtowers, then stockpile assault rifles, Ghillie suits, medical supplies, and military-grade ammunition.
  • The Ultimate Hunt: Search the mainland for every survivor and military outpost, confiscating anything remotely spherical because they believe Chernarus's high-tech containers, grenades, and glowing medical injectors are prototype Poké Balls hiding legendary regional beasts.

A Breach at the Gates

A deafening explosion shatters the quiet. Dust and concrete shrapnel rain down from the prison's outer security checkpoint as a heavily armored survivor in a Gorka suit, carrying a silenced M4A1, charges across the access bridge.

“Base is compromised! Some weirdos in clownish white uniforms are squatting in the prison! They've locked down all our high-tier loot!”

Jessie removes her mud-stained trench coat, reveals her pristine uniform, and strikes a dramatic pose. James scrambles to her side as they begin their routine:

  • Jessie: “Prepare for trouble!”
  • James: “And make it double!”
  • Jessie: “To protect the world from devastation...”
  • James: “To unite all peoples within our nation!”
  • Jessie: “To denounce the evils of truth and love...”
  • James: “To extend our reach to the stars above!”
  • Jessie: “Jessie!”
  • James: “And James!”
  • Jessie: “Team Rocket, blast off at the speed of light!”
  • James: “Surrender now, or prepare to fight—with high-caliber military hardware!”
  • Meowth: “Meowth, that's right!”

The survivor freezes in confusion at the theatrical monologue echoing through the prison walls. Jessie grins, chambers a round in her stolen VSS Vintorez, and announces:

“Well, look what the infection dragged in. Another test subject for Giovanni's private zoo!”

The siege is coming. Prepare for the next operation.

Team Rocket PvP Event · Completed

Two operatives. Two targets.

Two live Team Rocket operatives have infiltrated Northwest Airfield. They are real BTA staff members playing under different names, with no admin abilities, god mode, teleporting, or staff advantages.

They are playing under the same conditions as everyone else. They can bleed, be knocked unconscious, run out of ammunition, and be killed.

Find them, kill them, and take their loot. They are armed, mobile, and carrying valuable Team Rocket supplies. They can run, hide, and fight back.

Both operatives are somewhere within Northwest Airfield. Their general locations are marked on your map, but the markers do not reveal their exact positions.

Remember: NWAF is a PvP zone. Other survivors are hunting them too, and everything carried by an operative is fair game once they are down.

The hunt is complete. Thank you to everyone who participated.
