Team Rocket PvP Event · Completed

Two operatives. Two targets.

Two live Team Rocket operatives have infiltrated Northwest Airfield. They are real BTA staff members playing under different names, with no admin abilities, god mode, teleporting, or staff advantages.

They are playing under the same conditions as everyone else. They can bleed, be knocked unconscious, run out of ammunition, and be killed.

Find them, kill them, and take their loot. They are armed, mobile, and carrying valuable Team Rocket supplies. They can run, hide, and fight back.

Both operatives are somewhere within Northwest Airfield. Their general locations are marked on your map, but the markers do not reveal their exact positions.

Remember: NWAF is a PvP zone. Other survivors are hunting them too, and everything carried by an operative is fair game once they are down.

The hunt is complete. Thank you to everyone who participated.
