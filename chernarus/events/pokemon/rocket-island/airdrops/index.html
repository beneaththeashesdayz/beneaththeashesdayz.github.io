The Siege of Rocket Island

Team Rocket Airdrops

Team Rocket airdrops have returned. These drops contain Pokéballs, Pokémon card booster packs, and ingredients needed to build the explosives required to breach Prison Island.

Multiple waves of explosives will be needed to break through the prison defenses. Teaming up with other survivors and making a plan of attack is strongly recommended.

Recommended plan: collect keys, track the airdrops, gather ingredients and coordinate your assault team.
