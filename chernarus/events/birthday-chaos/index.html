BTA Birthday Chaos

Beneath the Ashes has officially survived its first year. Birthday Chaos has arrived across Chernarus from September 13 through September 30, 2026. Collect birthday items, chase special airdrops, and complete collection tiers before the event ends.

Birthday Collection Challenge

Collect and turn in.

Birthday Cake Slices spawn naturally while looting and can also be found in Birthday Airdrops.

Celebratory Beer does not spawn naturally. It can only be obtained from Birthday Airdrops.

Ashen Birthday Airdrops

Three tiers of chaos.

Higher tier means a rarer drop and better potential loot. All three tiers may contain Birthday Cake Slices and Celebratory Beer.

Tier 1 · Common

Ashen Birthday Drop

Requires a Kitchen Timer to unlock.

Tier 2 · Less common

Ashen Birthday Bash Drop

Better potential loot. Requires a Can Opener to unlock.

Tier 3 · Rarest

Ashen Birthday Jackpot Drop

Best potential loot. Requires Handcuff Keys to unlock.

Yes, a Kitchen Timer, Can Opener, and Handcuff Keys are apparently valid airdrop security devices. No, we are not explaining why. Each item only opens its corresponding drop; they are not interchangeable.

Before September 30

Open your Discord ticket.

Each tier can be claimed once, tiers do not have to be completed in order, and turn-in items are consumed. When you are ready, open a Discord ticket for staff verification and complete your desired tier before the event ends.
