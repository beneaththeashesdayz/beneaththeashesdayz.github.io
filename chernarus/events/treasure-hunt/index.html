The Governor's Great Treasure Hunt

Attention, adventurers! Somewhere across the wild lands lies a treasure worthy of the bravest scavengers. The treasure has been scattered behind nine mysterious clues. Solve each riddle, find the location, and uncover the next piece of the trail.

LIVE EVENT
Treasure Stash

The treasure awaits.

There is one treasure stash per group. Once your group completes the hunt, share your findings and screenshots in Discord so everyone can celebrate the discovery.

The Hunt

Nine clues. One treasure.

Read carefully, race to each location, and do not spoil the next clue.

Clue 01

The Lonely Marker

“Where the frozen winds blow at North-West,
A numbered sentinel stands among the rest.
Seek the marker bearing 180 degrees times two.”

Clue 02

The Broken Beast

“Once it carried travelers, now it carries none,
Its wheels are still, its journey is done.
Where Fort Kamensk holds secrets untold,
Find the broken truck rusting old.”

Clue 03

The Numbers Between

“Two numbers guard the path ahead,
One four-five-four, one four-six-three is said.
But don’t seek either one—that’s the trick!
Search between them, and you’ll find it quick!”

Clue 04

The Three Watchers

FOUND

“Three silent watchers gaze at the sky,
Upon a mountain standing high.
They mark the hill where secrets sleep,
Climb toward the crosses—the trail you keep.”

Clue 05

The Iron Giant

“Where industry sleeps beneath skies of gray,
An iron beast waits, silent all day.
Its mighty blade has stopped its roar,
Stand in front of the bulldozer and search the floor!”

Clue 06

The Iron Twins

“Two iron giants stand side by side,
Their journeys ended, nowhere to ride.
In Greenville West, where the old rails run,
Look behind the engines—your hunt isn’t done!”

Clue 07

The Woodland Campfire

“Leave the rails and enter the trees,
Follow the whispers carried by the breeze.
Near Old Salem, where the woodland grows,
A lonely campsite hides what nobody knows.”

Clue 08

The U of Doom

“A strange metal shape awaits your eyes,
It bends like a U beneath the skies.
Fort Paulding holds this twisted prize,
Find the U-shaped pipe—your next clue lies nearby!”

Clue 09

The Final Runway

“Your final journey takes you where the birds don’t fly,
Where machines once thundered across the sky.
At Redwood Regional, search with care,
A tiny runway light will lead you there.”
