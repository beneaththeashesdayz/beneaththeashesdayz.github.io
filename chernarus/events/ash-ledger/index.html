01 // The Mystery

Some Secrets Were Never Meant to Survive

Some secrets were never meant to survive the apocalypse.

Buried beneath years of lies, forgotten records, and carefully constructed death reports lies a story that someone desperately wants erased.

But the dead have a way of leaving things behind.

A name. A document. A transmission that should never have been recorded.

And somewhere in Chernarus, there are people who remember more than they’re willing to admit.

The Ash Ledger is now open. An immersive, story-driven questline awaits those brave—or foolish—enough to follow the trail.

02 // Where It Begins

Your Journey Starts in Reedsville

Mara Varga — The Keeper

Seek out a woman known as Mara Varga — The Keeper in Reedsville.

She has a story to tell.

Whether you believe her is entirely up to you.

03 // A Few Words of Advice

Read Carefully. Follow the Trail.

Read Everything

Every document you receive may contain information essential to your journey. Read both the front and back of every piece of paperwork. Overlooking a single detail could leave you wondering where to go next.

No Convenient Map Markers

Don’t expect someone to hold your hand. There are no convenient map markers pointing you toward your next destination. Pay attention to conversations, study your clues, and follow the trail.

Come Prepared

You’re not the only one interested in these records. Armed HOLLOW operatives have been spotted throughout Chernarus, and they aren’t particularly fond of people asking questions.

Discover the Story

Some things are better discovered than explained. Take your time. Follow the story. You might be surprised by what you uncover.

04 // Is It Worth the Trouble?

You Won’t Walk Away Empty-Handed

Let’s just say those who make it to the end won’t walk away empty-handed.

A special reward awaits survivors who complete the entire questline.

What is it? You’ll have to find out.

05 // Availability

Available All November Long

This questline is available once per player, throughout November 2026.

You have the entire month to unravel the mystery, but whether you’ll like what you discover is another question entirely.
