Halloween Haul

The Dead Have Brought Treats.

Something wicked has awakened in Chernarus. The skies are turning purple, the dead are crawling from their graves, and mysterious Halloween supply crates are falling across the wasteland!

LIVE EVENT
Chernarus Only
Event Ends: Sunday, November 1, 2026 — 11:59 PM Eastern Loading countdown…
01 // Follow the signs

Purple Smoke Means Trouble

The next haul does not arrive quietly. Watch the sky, listen for the warning and move when the purple starts rolling in.

Hourly Halloween Airdrops

  • Special Halloween airdrops arrive every hour, separate from regular airdrops.
  • Survivors receive advance warnings before a drop arrives.
  • Look for the distinctive purple smoke and purple map markers.
  • Each Halloween crate remains available for a limited time.

Search the Purple Circle

The purple search circle marks the area where players should search for the Halloween crate. It is a search clue, not a promise that the crate is sitting in the exact center waiting politely.

02 // Trick or treat

Or Die Trying!

The treats have guardians, and the guardians are having a terrible night.

Halloween Infected

  • Pumpkin-headed infected
  • Ancient mummies
  • Four different witch variants

Bring a Crew

Dangerous infected guard the Halloween crates, and opening a crate can bring another wave of horrors. Bring weapons, ammunition, medical supplies and friends who run slightly slower than you do.

03 // The key to the haul

The Mystery of the Violet Key

Every good Halloween mystery needs a key. This one is purple, useful and absolutely not something to leave in a corpse.

Unlock the Crates

  • Halloween crates require a Violet Key.
  • A key can be obtained from infected guarding each Halloween airdrop.
  • Violet Keys can also occasionally be found while hunting ordinary infected across Chernarus.
  • The key is consumed when a crate is successfully opened.
Prepare for the next drop: Hunt infected between airdrops, keep your Violet Keys safe and be ready when the purple smoke appears.
