The hunt

Bring their heads home.

The objective is simple: hunt dragons, claim their trophies, and make your mark.

Every Dragon Head Trophy collected should be physically displayed at the player’s base. Hang them on walls, build a trophy room, or create whatever kind of dragon trophy display you want.

At the end of the event, the players with the most qualifying Dragon Head Trophies will claim 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.

Event rules

Make the trophies count.

Staff may verify leading displays before prizes are awarded.

  • Hunt dragons throughout Chernarus and collect Dragon Head Trophies.
  • Dragon Head Trophies must be physically displayed at your base to count toward your final total.
  • Post pictures of your trophy display in the Hall of Dragon Slayers Discord thread.
  • Post updated pictures throughout the event as your collection grows.
  • Final placement is determined by the number of qualifying Dragon Head Trophies displayed when the event ends.
  • Inventory screenshots or trophies simply dropped in piles on the ground do not count. Trophies must be properly displayed.
  • Staff may verify the trophy displays of leading hunters before prizes are awarded.
  • You do not need to compete for the top three positions to participate. Everyone is encouraged to share their displays.
