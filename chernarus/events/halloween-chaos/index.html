Halloween Has Come to Chernarus

Halloween has taken over Chernarus. The hunt is live. Strange creatures are moving through the world, seasonal loot is waiting to be found, and the safest way across the map may now involve four legs.

🎃 Halloween Loot

  • Basic Halloween gear and Halloween teddies can be found throughout Chernarus.
  • Halloween snacks can also be found around the world.
  • Special Halloween Teddy Barrels provide storage specifically for Halloween teddies.

🔥 Dragons

Dragons can now be found around the castles of Chernarus. Skinning a dragon gives you a chance to receive military Halloween loot, along with higher-tier and special Halloween teddies that are not part of ordinary world loot.

👀 A Mystery Creature

A strange and spooky creature has appeared somewhere in Chernarus. No one is saying exactly where it is or what it leaves behind. Discover it naturally if you can.

🐴 Horses

Wild horses are now available in Chernarus. Keep an eye out for saddles, bridles and horse bags, then learn how to claim, ride, stable and retrieve your horse.

🧪 Halloween Inventory

The inventory UI has received a Halloween-themed seasonal appearance. Even checking your supplies feels a little different this time of year.

Sharpen your blades. Load your magazines. Gather your crew.
Keep your eyes open, listen carefully, and remember: Chernarus is feeling a lot less ordinary this Halloween.
