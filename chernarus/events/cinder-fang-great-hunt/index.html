Legendary hunts

Watch for the bounty.

Throughout the event, Cinder & Fang will issue rotating Legendary Bounties on specific animals.

Each Legendary Hunt will run for approximately two hours. During an active hunt, the selected animal will be worth bonus points, with the multiplier varying depending on the animal.

The Legendary Hunt schedule will not be published ahead of time. Watch Discord for each new bounty announcement.

Legendary turn-in rules

Turn in before the window closes.

Legendary bonuses are tied to their specific active hunt window.

To receive bonus points, qualifying animals must be turned in to staff before that Legendary Hunt ends.

Once the Legendary window closes, those pelts may still be turned in toward your overall event score, but they receive only their normal base point value. No late Legendary turn-ins receive bonus points.

Rules

Hunt smart.

Only pelts are accepted for animal turn-ins, with the exception of chickens, which must be turned in whole.

Points accumulate throughout the entire event. Rewards are based on the highest tier reached.

Legendary bonuses only apply to qualifying animals turned in during their active Legendary Hunt window. Once a window closes, those animals return to their normal point value.

Open a Discord ticket when you are ready to make a turn-in so staff can verify and record your points.

All final qualifying turn-ins must be completed before MIDNIGHT EDT on Sunday, September 20, 2026.

This is not a leaderboard competition. There is no 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place. Multiple players can earn the same reward tier.
