Turn-in system

How reward tiers work.

Read this before you spend a trophy.

01

Any order

Complete Tier 1, Tier 3, or Tier 5 first. There is no required progression path.

02

Once per player

Each reward tier may be completed and claimed once per player.

03

Trophies are consumed

Animals and pelts used for a tier are consumed when turned in.

04

Used points cannot return

Points spent on one tier cannot be reused toward another tier.

05

All five can be earned

Want every reward package? You can claim all five tiers, but you need 1,750 total points across separate qualifying turn-ins.

Example: Turn in 300 points to claim Tier 3. Those 300 points are spent. If you later want Tier 1, you must collect and turn in another 50 points worth of qualifying animals.
Rotating bounties

Legendary Hunts.

Temporary targets. Bigger points. No published schedule.

The bounty

Watch Discord.

Throughout the event, Cinder & Fang issues rotating Legendary Bounties on specific animals. The selected animal receives bonus points during the active hunt.

The window

Approximately two hours.

Each Legendary Hunt lasts about two hours. The bonus multiplier varies depending on the animal.

The schedule

No warning calendar.

The Legendary Hunt schedule is not published ahead of time. Keep an eye on Discord for every new bounty announcement.

Legendary turn-in rules

Bonus points expire with the hunt.

Legendary bonuses only apply when the qualifying animals are turned in to staff before that specific Legendary Hunt ends.

Before the window closesQualifying animals receive the active bonus multiplier.
After the window closesThe animals are still valid for normal turn-ins, but return to their normal base point value.
No late bonusNo late Legendary turn-in receives bonus points.
General event rules

Bring the right trophies.

Everything staff needs to verify your claim.

Turn-ins

Open a Discord ticket when you are ready to turn in. Staff will verify the items, calculate the points, record the completed tier, and provide the reward.

Accepted items

Only pelts are accepted for normal animal turn-ins, except chickens, which must be turned in whole.

Consumed trophies

Turned-in animals and pelts are consumed. A tier's points cannot be used again.

Order and claims

Tiers may be completed in any order. Each tier may only be claimed once per player.

No leaderboard

There is no 1st, 2nd, or 3rd place. Multiple players can earn every reward tier.

Final deadline

All final qualifying turn-ins must be completed before midnight EDT on Sunday, September 20, 2026.
