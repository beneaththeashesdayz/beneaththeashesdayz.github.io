Trader Lore

Linda learned early that in Chernarus, a clean bandage can be worth more than a loaded magazine when things go wrong. Her counter at Scorched Isle Market has become the place survivors visit when their packs are overflowing with medical loot, or dangerously empty of it.

She buys the supplies survivors can spare and puts useful medical stock back into circulation. It is a simple system, but one that keeps medicine moving through the mainland instead of gathering dust in forgotten stashes.

Whether you are clearing out excess supplies after a hospital run or preparing for the next ugly trip north, Linda keeps the market's medical shelves ready.

Chernarus is remarkably good at hurting people. Linda makes sure you can do something about it.
