Trader Lore

Peter has little patience for survivors who treat the wilderness as scenery. To him, the forests of Chernarus are a pantry, a workshop, and a paycheck, provided you know what you are doing.

From Cinder & Fang, he trades in the practical remains of the hunt. Meat keeps people alive. Pelts and rugs carry value long after the fire burns out. Animal heads are proof that somebody went into the wild, found something worth tracking, and came back standing.

Peter also keeps a small selection of equipment moving through his counter. Crossbows, hunting bolts, and hunting knives are tools he is willing to buy back from survivors and sell to the next hunter preparing to head out.

He is not interested in being a general weapons dealer. If it does not belong in the hunt, it probably does not belong on his counter.

For survivors willing to earn their money beyond the roads and towns, Peter makes sure a good hunt never goes to waste.
