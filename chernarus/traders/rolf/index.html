Trader Lore

Before the Collapse, Rolf always had a talent for knowing what things were worth.

More importantly, he knew what people thought they were worth.

When ordinary money became little more than kindling, Rolf adapted faster than most. He watched new currencies rise and disappear, settlements invent their own systems, and survivors trade everything from ammunition to canned peaches just to avoid walking away empty-handed.

Then came the Zombie Notes.

Nobody seems entirely sure when Rolf started dealing in them. Ask him, and you'll get a grin, a shrug, and probably a worse exchange rate than you were hoping for.

These days, Rolf operates out of Scorched Isle Market as the mainland's Zombie Note trader. Survivors arrive carrying pockets full of strange valuables and leave with considerably fewer questions and, if Rolf has done his job properly, considerably fewer valuables.

He'll buy what interests him. He'll tell you what doesn't. And somehow, no matter how much business passes across his counter, there is always another battered stack of notes tucked inside that jacket.

Fishing gear is one of the few things that genuinely catches his attention. Certain pieces pulled from the waters around Chernarus can't be bought from him at all. If you've managed to fish one up, though, Rolf is willing to pay for it.

Where it goes afterward?

Rolf just smiles.

His rule is simple: “If you're asking what it's worth, I've already got the better hand.”
