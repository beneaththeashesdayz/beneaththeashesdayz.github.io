Trader Lore

Cinder Market has never been a place for ordinary shopping lists. Its Zombie Note counter exists for survivors who arrive with stranger currency and considerably larger ambitions.

The shelves are a rotating collection of specialist equipment, custom gear, workbench hardware and other pieces that rarely belong in a mainland storefront. The catalogue changes with the live market, and the rules of each category matter just as much as the price printed beside it.

Fishing gear has its own place in the arrangement. Certain pieces are valuable precisely because they cannot simply be ordered across the counter, giving survivors another reason to bring unusual catches and hard-earned equipment out to Cinder.

At Cinder Market, Zombie Notes are less a currency than proof that you have survived long enough to shop differently.
