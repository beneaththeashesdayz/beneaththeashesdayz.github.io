The Story of Graves

Before Cinder Market, Graves was said to have worked around the Ember Institute, moving sealed crates and handwritten reports between the laboratories and the coast. He never says exactly what his job was. He only remembers that the researchers were studying the dead—and that the dead eventually found their way inside.

When the Institute went dark, he came out carrying a battered ledger and a handful of coded notes. He also came out infected. The fever took him, the sickness turned him, and somehow the thing that returned still remembered the routes, the numbers and the value of a well-placed bargain.

Some survivors believe he escaped through the lower service tunnels before he turned. Others think the Institute kept him alive—or brought him back—for reasons no one has uncovered. Now he runs the black-market counter at Cinder Market, trading specialist equipment, workbenches, mag loaders and rare supplies through channels that do not appear on any mainland map.

If you want answers about Ember, the trader may know where they are buried—but remember that the man taking your Zombie Notes is one of the dead who learned how to keep talking.
