The Story of Silas

Before Cinder Market, Silas was said to have worked around the Ember Institute, moving sealed crates and handwritten reports between the laboratories and the coast. He never says exactly what his job was. He only remembers that the researchers were studying the dead—and that the dead eventually found their way inside.

When the Center went dark, Silas came out carrying a battered ledger, a handful of coded notes and the habit of never asking where useful equipment came from. Some survivors believe he escaped through the lower service tunnels. Others say the Center let him go because he knew something it could not afford to lose.

Now he runs the black-market counter at Cinder Market. His shelves hold the kind of gear that keeps expeditions alive: specialist equipment, workbenches, mag loaders and supplies traded through channels that do not appear on any mainland map. Silas does not offer explanations with every purchase. He offers a price, a warning and a reason to come back prepared.

If you want answers about Ember, Silas may know where they are buried—but he will make you pay in Zombie Notes before he points you toward them.
