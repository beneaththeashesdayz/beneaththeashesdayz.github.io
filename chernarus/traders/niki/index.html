Trader Lore

Before the outbreak, Niki spent most of his life keeping other people's machines alive.

He worked in fleet maintenance, the kind of job where a vehicle was never allowed to become sentimental. Vans, utility trucks, old sedans, service vehicles, whatever rolled into the yard went onto a lift, got stripped down, repaired, and sent back out before anyone had time to complain about the bill. Niki learned quickly that most breakdowns were not dramatic. They were a belt ignored too long, a battery pushed past its last winter, a hose nobody thought worth replacing.

When Chernarus collapsed, that knowledge became far more valuable than any certificate hanging in a workshop office.

Niki moved between survivor groups for years, repairing whatever they could drag, tow, or limp into camp. He rarely asked for much in return. Fuel. Food. A dry place to work. What mattered to him was keeping vehicles moving, because he had seen too many people die after a truck failed at the wrong moment.

His attitude hardened into a rule: basic mechanical survival should not be a luxury.

By the time he reached Scorched Isle Market, Niki had already accumulated crates of salvaged parts and a reputation for giving away the small things other mechanics would hoard. Sparky's growing vehicle operation finally gave him a permanent home. Sparky puts survivors behind the wheel; Niki makes sure a bad radiator, ruined battery, or missing component does not turn that purchase into an expensive lawn ornament.

He still charges for the things he considers optional. Underglow is style, not survival. Personal garages are convenience. Those can cost a little.

But bring him a vehicle that simply needs to stay alive, and Niki's answer is usually the same.

“Take the part. Keep the car moving.”
