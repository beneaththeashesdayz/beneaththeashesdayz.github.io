Trader Lore

Long after roads became dangerous and supermarkets became empty shells, the water kept producing.

Keiko built The Grey Current around that simple fact. Fish do not care who controls a town, which road is blocked, or what currency survived the collapse. If you know where to cast and have the patience to wait, the water will still give you something worth carrying home.

Her counter handles nearly every part of that trade. Whole fish, prepared meat, fishing equipment, and the specialized Geb's Fish goods all have a place here. Survivors who live by the water can turn their catch into steady income, then restock before heading out again.

Keiko keeps the operation practical. No spectacle, no inflated promises. Bring something pulled from the water or something useful for getting it there, and she probably knows exactly what it is worth.

For survivors who would rather trust a fishing line than a firefight, The Grey Current keeps the money moving with the tide.
