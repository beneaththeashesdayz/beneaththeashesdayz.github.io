Trader Lore

Some people survived the collapse by learning how to shoot. Others learned how to disappear. Hassan learned how to keep a supply chain alive after the world stopped having one.

He built his trade around a simple rule: unfinished work is a liability. Anyone can drag loose ingredients across Chernarus. Hassan pays for the people willing to finish the job, package it, and bring him something ready to move.

That is why finished and properly bundled goods carry the most weight at his counter. He will take certain individually bundled products, finished LSD, and cannabis nuggets as well, but Hassan has little patience for half-completed production. Clean product, packaged properly, means fewer questions and better business.

From his operation at Cinder Market, Hassan also supplies the tools that keep that business moving. Zip-lock baggies, production kits, growing stations, and the equipment needed to turn scattered materials into finished goods all pass through his hands.

Reaching him is part of the price. Cinder Market sits out in the Eastern Sea, far enough from the mainland that casual traders rarely make the trip. Those who do usually arrive with a reason.

Hassan does not care how difficult the run was. He cares whether you brought something worth buying.
