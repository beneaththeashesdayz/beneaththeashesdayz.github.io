Trader Lore

Gabi believes every relic has a story. Kaito is considerably more interested in what that story is worth.

Operating from Cinder Market, Kaito built his reputation as the collector survivors seek out when convenience matters less than profit. Drippy sneakers, Fallout memorabilia, bobbleheads, Nuka-Cola pieces, Paragon and Vyse collectables, and sealed Pokémon boxes all move across his counter.

He pays considerably more than mainland buyers, but the difference is not generosity. Cinder Market is isolated in the Eastern Sea, and anyone arriving with valuable cargo has already accepted the risk and expense of reaching him. Kaito understands exactly what that journey adds to the value of a deal.

Unlike Gabi, he keeps no shelves of storage supplies and offers nothing back across the counter. Kaito is a buyer, not a shopkeeper. Survivors come to him to turn rare finds into serious money, then leave with lighter packs and heavier pockets.

If the collection is valuable enough to protect all the way to Cinder Market, Kaito is usually willing to make the trip worthwhile.
