Trader Lore

Before the collapse, Indar repaired heavy equipment for men who cared more about keeping machines alive than keeping people happy. When the roads emptied and the work stopped coming, he discovered that the same patience worked just as well on damaged weapons.

He spent the first winter moving from settlement to settlement with a canvas roll of tools, trading repairs for food and stripping ruined gear for anything that could still be useful. Survivors began remembering the quiet man who could look at a battered rifle or bent blade and tell you, almost immediately, whether it was worth saving.

By the time Scorched Isle Market began drawing regular traffic, Indar had already built a reputation for refusing bad deals and worse weapons. He eventually claimed a counter of his own, but never turned it into an arsenal. The most dangerous hardware rarely stays on his shelves long, and the guns he sells are chosen for reliability rather than bragging rights.

He speaks little about the years before the market, and even less about the scar running across his left hand. Ask where it came from and he usually changes the subject.

Around Scorched Isle, survivors have a saying: if Indar says a weapon is worth carrying, you probably want it when the road turns ugly.
