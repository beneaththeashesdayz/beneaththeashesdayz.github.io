Trader Lore

Indar runs the kind of counter that attracts survivors carrying more steel than food. From Scorched Isle Market, he keeps weapons, magazines, attachments and ammunition moving between the people who found too much and the people who do not have enough.

He buys broadly, including standard Chernarus firearms and equipment as well as My DF Weapons stock. If a gun, magazine, attachment or box of ammunition still has value, Indar is usually willing to put a number on it.

What he sells is more deliberate. His shelves are built around practical starter-tier firearms rather than the server's most powerful hardware, giving survivors a dependable place to gear up without turning the mainland market into an end-game arsenal.

Indar does not promise the biggest gun in Chernarus. He promises something you can actually use when the next fight finds you.
