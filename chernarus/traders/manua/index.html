Trader Lore

Long before Chernarus became a graveyard of half-finished fortifications, Manua made his living restoring places other people had already given up on.

He worked construction crews from the coast to the northern industrial towns, moving wherever there was a roof sagging, a generator coughing its last breath, or an old building somebody insisted could still be saved. He was never the loudest man on a job site, but he was usually the one people called when the first plan failed.

When the outbreak began, Manua did what he had always done: he started fixing things.

At first it was barricades. Then gates. Then makeshift power systems, reinforced shelters and entire safehouses stitched together from whatever could be stripped out of abandoned workshops and warehouses. For a while, he travelled with a small group of survivors who believed they could keep moving forever. They were wrong.

One winter, a settlement they had spent months building was overrun while Manua was away scavenging electrical parts. By the time he returned, there was nothing left worth repairing.

He stopped building homes after that.

Instead, he began collecting the pieces other survivors needed to build their own. Locks. Batteries. Wiring. Storage. Tools. Lighting. Anything that could turn four exposed walls into somewhere a person might survive the night. Eventually his stockpile grew too large to carry and too useful to hide, and Manua settled into Scorched Isle Market as its building supplies trader.

He still knows exactly how every piece on his shelves is meant to fit together. He just prefers not to be the one deciding where the walls should go anymore.

Ask him why and he will usually shrug. “I build the parts now. You build the place.”
