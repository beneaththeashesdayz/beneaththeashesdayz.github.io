Trader Lore

Every settlement starts the same way: somebody decides a patch of ruined ground is worth defending.

Manua built his trade around that decision. His stock moves from the practical to the oddly domestic, from locks, batteries and power-grid components to storage, lights, sleeping bags and the furnishings that make a fortified shell feel almost like somewhere a person could live.

He deals heavily in BaseBuildingPlus equipment and keeps a steady flow of essential construction supplies moving through Scorched Isle Market. Survivors come to him before the first wall goes up, and usually return when the generator dies, the storage fills, or somebody realizes the base still needs a light over the workbench.

Some tools, however, never make it onto his sale racks. Pickaxes, hacksaws and sledgehammers carry too much value outside ordinary construction. Manua will buy them, but anyone wanting one has to earn it the old-fashioned way: go out into Chernarus and find it.
