Trader Lore

Before the Collapse, Naomi spent years working around food distribution. Not the glamorous end of it. Warehouses, deliveries, stock counts and the endless little decisions that kept shelves full while everyone else simply assumed they would stay that way.

That assumption vanished quickly.

When supply chains broke, Naomi watched settlements make the same mistake again and again: they planned for gunfights and forgot about breakfast. Ammunition was guarded like treasure while tins spoiled in flooded storerooms, clean drinks disappeared, and perfectly good seed packets were left behind because nobody had room in their pack.

She started collecting what other survivors overlooked. Food that would travel. Drinks that would keep. Seeds that could become something more valuable than another meal scavenged from a kitchen cupboard.

Eventually Naomi found her way to Scorched Isle Market, where her old habits became useful again. She keeps the shelves stocked with the consumables survivors need today, while buying garden lime and vegetable seeds from anyone willing to bring them in. She does not resell those gardening supplies. To Naomi, they are part of something bigger: keeping useful growing stock moving back into the hands and systems that can keep Chernarus fed.

Her rule is simple: “You can survive a firefight in five minutes. Winter takes months.”
