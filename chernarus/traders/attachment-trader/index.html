Survivor Notes

  • Most listed categories are two-way trade. You can buy them from the trader and sell accepted items back.
  • Standard sell-back pricing follows the live server market configuration and is currently calculated from the configured market sell percentage unless an item has its own override.

  • The Batteries category is intentionally hidden from normal market browsing and is not included in this catalogue.
