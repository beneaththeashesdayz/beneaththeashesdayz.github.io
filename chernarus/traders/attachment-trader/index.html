Deputy Judy

Judy has never understood why survivors insist on calling her counter a shop. As far as she's concerned, it's an armory with a cash register.

Working under Sheriff Indar at Scorched Isle Market, Deputy Judy keeps a close eye on the attachments, magazines, optics and ammunition moving through the settlement. Indar handles the firearms. Judy handles everything survivors inevitably bolt onto them five minutes later.

She knows her inventory, knows what fits what, and has very little patience for anyone who doesn't. Ask a sensible question and she'll get you squared away. Spend twenty minutes debating which optic looks cooler and you'll eventually notice her staring at you over the counter.

Despite the attitude, Judy takes the job seriously. Every magazine counted, every attachment accounted for, every transaction handled properly. In a place where armed survivors wander through the gates every day, Indar needs someone he can trust behind that counter.

Judy is that someone. Just don't ask if she has anything “in the back.”
