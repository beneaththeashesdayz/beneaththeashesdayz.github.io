Deputy Tess Archer

Tess Archer has never understood why survivors insist on calling her counter a shop. As far as she's concerned, it's an armory with a cash register.

Working under Sheriff Jonas Flint at Scorched Isle Market, she keeps a close eye on the attachments, magazines, optics and ammunition moving through the settlement. The sheriff handles the firearms. His deputy handles everything survivors inevitably bolt onto them five minutes later.

She knows her inventory, knows what fits what, and has very little patience for anyone who doesn't. Ask a sensible question and she'll get you squared away. Spend twenty minutes debating which optic looks cooler and you'll eventually notice her staring at you over the counter.

Despite the attitude, Tess takes the job seriously. Every magazine counted, every attachment accounted for, every transaction handled properly. In a place where armed survivors wander through the gates every day, Jonas needs someone he can trust behind that counter.

She is that someone. Just don't ask if she has anything “in the back.”
